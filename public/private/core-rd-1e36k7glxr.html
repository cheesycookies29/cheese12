02 · The modules

Seven parts. One system.

Each team works in its own module; the records connect underneath. Scroll through them.

01
MOD-01

Human Resource Management

From the first clock-in to the payslip, on one set of data.

  • Employee records with government IDs and bank details masked; every reveal is logged
  • Time clock on the desk or a shared kiosk; times from the server, never the device
  • Leave, work from home and official business, approved on routes of up to three levels
  • Payroll from the attendance: SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG and BIR, payslips and year to date
MOD-02

IT Assets

Every laptop, phone and box of toner, and who has it.

  • A register of equipment with tags, serials, warranty and history
  • Assign and return, with the condition and a record of each hand-over
  • Consumable stock received and issued, with low-stock warnings
  • Employees report a broken laptop from their own page
MOD-03

Service Desk

Tickets from the portal, an email inbox and a public form, in one queue.

  • Its own email engine: reads the desk's mailbox and threads every reply
  • Service levels per priority, with what is close to breaching
  • Saved replies, internal notes, assignment and categories
  • A ticket becomes a project task in one click
MOD-04

Sales & CRM

Leads to deals to contracts, with government bids built in.

  • A pipeline board of deals by stage, value and close date
  • Bids with the document checklist government tenders ask for
  • Contracts, accounts, contacts and every call and meeting
  • A deal won starts its delivery project
MOD-05

Projects

Boards, timelines and the time it really took.

  • Board, list, table and timeline views, with saved filters
  • Milestones, burn-up charts, effort sizes and load per person
  • Timesheets, comments with a visual editor, files and mentions
  • Members-only projects: nobody else sees them
MOD-06 · 07

Admin & Security

Who may do what, what changed, and how the system is holding up.

  • 10 account types and 39 permissions, checked on the server for every request
  • A settings center with each change's history and undo
  • A sealed audit log that shows when entries were edited or removed
  • A security monitor: status checks, refused requests, traffic and who is signed in

And for everyone

  • One homeA card for each module a person may open, with live figures.
  • Self-serviceClock in, file leave, see payslips, report equipment, follow tickets.
  • One bellEvery module's notifications and approvals in one list.
  • 11 guidesOne per kind of user, with 118 screenshots, kept current with each release.

04 · Made for the Philippines

The payslip, worked out.

Philippine contributions and BIR withholding are built in, and the rates are settings, not code: when the law changes, Payroll Setup changes with it.

  • SSS2025 schedule: employee 5%, employer 10% of the monthly salary credit (₱5,000 to ₱35,000)
  • PhilHealth5% of basic pay between ₱10,000 and ₱100,000, shared half and half
  • Pag-IBIG2% from the employee and 2% from the employer, up to ₱200 each
  • BIRTRAIN withholding table effective 2023; minimum wage earners exempt
  • PremiumsHolidays at 130%, 200% and 300%; rest days; night differential of 10%
  • Calendars2026 and 2027 proclaimed holidays loaded; HR adds Eid and local days
PayslipMonthly · illustrative
Net pay₱0.00

Worked out with CORE's built-in rates for a monthly-paid employee with no absences, premiums or other deductions. A real payslip comes from the attendance.

05 · Security
