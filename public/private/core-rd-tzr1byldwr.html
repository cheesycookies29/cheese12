Why Legati

A legatus carried the authority of Rome and answered for how it was used. We hold every company in the group to that standard: strength and stability, carried with authority and responsibility.

lēgātusLatin · plural lēgātī

An envoy or commander entrusted to act for Rome. Our mark is drawn from the meander that ran along Roman pillars, with the letters L and G inside it.

What the group provides

A holding company earns its place by making each business stronger than it would be alone. These are the three things we give every company in the group.

Strategic direction

The group sets the long-term course for each business and weighs decisions against the next ten years, not the next quarter.

Corporate oversight

Each company keeps its own leadership, name and customers. The group sets the standards, supports the people who lead, and holds them to account.

Shared services

Finance, legal and compliance, communications and human resources are led at group level, so each company can give its attention to its customers.

What it builds

  • Collaboration
  • Operational alignment
  • Sustainable growth

Chairman & CEO

He has led Legati Group’s companies to consistent year-on-year growth while spearheading digital transformation initiatives across the country.

A businessman by profession, John has built and invested in ventures spanning the automotive, food and beverage, infrastructure, information technology and real estate sectors, and he continues to steer his companies toward market leadership and long-term value creation. He graduated cum laude from De La Salle University and earned his postgraduate degree in Management from the Asian Institute of Management.
