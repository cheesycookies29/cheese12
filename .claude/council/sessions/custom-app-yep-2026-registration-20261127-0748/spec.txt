01 / SCOPE AND DATA

Registration & table assignment
Functional requirements and specifications for a mobile-friendly, web-based system that verifies guests,
recommends appropriate seating and gives designated personnel real-time control.


  Event date                                    Working capacity                         Hard limit

  9 December 2026                               300 attendees (planning assumption)      10 persons per table


1. Objective and core business rules
   Facilitate efficient arrival and check-in without requiring guests to install an app or create an account.
   Support networking by recommending tables based on industry or sector affinity, rather than filling tables
   sequentially.
   Allow designated registration personnel to choose the table and, where applicable, the individual seat.
   Enforce the 10-person capacity at all times, including reservations and simultaneous assignments.
   Provide exactly one Super Administrator with authority over all guests and tables; other personnel have
   exclusive, delegated scopes.

2. Master guest list
Import existing Excel or CSV guest records; allow authorized users to add, edit and update records. Use a
stable unique guest identifier to prevent duplicate registrations and assignments.

Required data: name, designation, company or organization, industry/sector, email (if available), mobile
number (if available), guest classification, RSVP status, designated registration personnel, table and seat (if
applicable), reservation status, registration status, check-in timestamp and notification status.

3. Attendee classifications

  Classification                      Assignment rule

  Preferred guests                    Retrieve preassigned table and seat on arrival.

  Regular guests                      Recommend industry-compatible eligible tables; personnel confirm selection.

  Employees                           Assign on arrival to eligible employee tables; personnel confirm selection.

  Unlisted attendees                  Authorized approval and classification before seating; route to Super Administrator.

02 / GUEST EXPERIENCE

Registration workflow
Separate arrival verification, seating confirmation and notification so staff can identify guests waiting for a table.


4. QR registration and identity verification
   Display a common QR code at reception that opens a mobile-friendly registration page. Allow administrators
   to activate/deactivate registration.
   Verify the attendee against the master list using a suitable identifier; provide staff-assisted partial-name
   search for discrepancies. Do not expose the master guest list publicly.
   Provide assisted check-in for guests without smartphones, data access or a usable email address.
   Prevent repeat check-ins and duplicate seat allocations. If identity cannot be verified, refer the guest for
   approval rather than automatically admitting them.

5. Processing and routing

  Route                             Workflow

  Preferred / express               Verify identity → check in → retrieve reserved table and seat → display and notify. If
                                    reservation was released, route for reassignment.

  Regular / employee                Verify identity → check in → route to designated personnel queue → review
                                    recommended tables → confirm seating → display and notify.

  Unlisted / exception              Flag for assistance → Super Administrator approves or rejects → record identity,
                                    classification and industry where applicable → delegate or assign → notify.

  No designated personnel           Route to Super Administrator queue for direct handling or delegation.


6. Notifications
   Show a pending-assignment status until seating is confirmed; refresh the guest-facing screen automatically.
   Immediately display the confirmed table number and seat number, where applicable. Automatically email the
   assignment when an email address is available.
   Allow updated notifications to be sent after changes, and allow staff to communicate assignments verbally.
   Track email success/failure separately; support resending without invalidating check-in or seating.

7. Exceptions and substitutions
Allow authorized staff to record representatives/substitutes with a link to the original invitee, transfer or release
the original reservation as approved, and prevent both records from consuming the same seat. Maintain an
approval and change history.

03 / ASSIGNMENT ENGINE

Seating recommendations
Recommendations are advisory; authorized personnel make the final selection. Availability and permissions are
mandatory constraints.


8. Table setup and capacity
   Configure table numbers, classifications and assigned personnel. Support preferred, regular, employee and
   mixed-classification tables if enabled by the Super Administrator.
   Count all confirmed assignments and active reservations toward the 10-person limit, even if the attendee has
   not arrived. Temporary holds, if implemented, must also block competing assignments.
   Never require one table to be filled before opening another. Hide or disable full tables for new assignments.
   If no eligible seat remains, alert personnel and refer to the Super Administrator; do not exceed the hard limit.

9. Industry-based ranking and selection
   Recommend multiple eligible tables, prioritized by same industry, related/complementary industry, sufficient
   capacity and other eligible tables when there is no match.
   Allow the Super Administrator to define industry categories and relationships. Optional seating tags or
   exclusions can account for cross-industry networking or sensitive pairings.
   Show recommended and other eligible tables, current assigned/reserved counts, remaining seats, assigned
   guest names, companies and industries, and reserved/available seat numbers as applicable.
   Permit personnel to choose a different eligible table or available seat regardless of the recommendation
   ranking. Limit write access to delegated tables.

10. Groups and preferred guests
   Allow staff to select multiple arriving guests and recommend tables with sufficient capacity for the entire
   group. If unavailable, present alternatives; split only with staff confirmation. Each member retains an
   individual registration and assignment.
   Keep existing preferred assignments unless an authorized change is approved. Groups spanning personnel
   scopes must be handled by the Super Administrator or formally delegated.
   Preferred no-show seats remain reserved until manually released by the Super Administrator or authorized
   delegate. No automatic expiry of preferred reservations. A late arrival after release must be referred for
   reassignment.

04 / PERMISSIONS

Exclusive responsibility model
Every guest and table has at most one designated registration personnel owner at a time. Only the Super
Administrator has unrestricted assignment authority.


11. Role-based access

  Role                                    Authority

  Attendee                                Register and view own status and seating assignment only.

  Designated registration                 Process only delegated guests; allocate seats only at delegated tables; view own
  personnel                               live queue and recommendations; perform authorized corrections within scope.

  Super Administrator (one                Access all guests and tables; delegate, reassign and revoke responsibilities;
  account)                                approve unlisted guests; resolve conflicts; perform cross-scope transfers; configure
                                          seating and permissions.


12. Exclusive guest and table delegation
   The Super Administrator assigns specific guests and specific tables to individual personnel before or during
   the event. A guest or table cannot be delegated to more than one person simultaneously.
   Route verified guests to their designated personnel in real time. Personnel see incoming registrations and
   pending assignments in their own queue.
   Limit recommendations and final selection to delegated eligible tables. A cross-scope assignment requires
   the Super Administrator to act directly or formally reallocate responsibility.
   If a personnel member is overloaded, absent, has no available seats, or experiences device failure, the
   Super Administrator can redistribute pending guests or tables without disturbing confirmed assignments.
   Permission changes take effect immediately; reject stale or unauthorized writes at the server, not just in the
   interface.

13. Accountability and visibility
   Keep an audit trail of approvals, manual overrides, responsibility changes, releases, transfers and
   corrections, with actor and timestamp.
   Recommended: staff may view overall table availability in read-only form to identify alternatives, but may not
   allocate seats outside their scope; restrict personal information to what is necessary.
   Provide secure recovery for the single Super Administrator account without creating a second unrestricted
   active account.

05 / ADMINISTRATION

Dashboard and controls
Real-time operational visibility and safe changes are essential throughout guest arrival and the event.


14. Dashboard
   Display total invited, RSVP-confirmed, checked-in, not-yet-arrived and approved unlisted guests; show
   check-ins by classification.
   Show pending verification/override, pending table assignment, assigned versus reserved seats, remaining
   capacity, full/available tables and industries represented at each table.
   Show personnel ownership, queue status and workload. Super Administrator sees all; personnel see their
   delegated records and permitted read-only summary.
   Prefer a visual table occupancy layout; make counts and reservations clear even without a floor-plan graphic.

15. Authorized administrative actions
   Add, deactivate, renumber or reclassify tables; prevent removal of a table with active assignments unless
   those assignments are safely relocated.
   Manage guest records, classifications, industry groups, ownership and reservations. Manually check in
   guests, correct erroneous check-ins and process group arrivals.
   Transfer guests between tables, swap assignments, release no-show reservations, and select a
   non-recommended eligible table. Cross-scope actions require Super Administrator authority.
   Approve unlisted attendees, record substitutions, resend notifications and search/filter by name,
   organization, industry, classification, registration status or table.
   Export guest, registration and seating records to Excel or CSV. Reflect changes immediately across affected
   devices. Confirm destructive changes and support safe correction/reversal subject to current capacity.

16. Transaction safeguards
   Revalidate permissions, reservation/seat status and table capacity immediately before confirmation. Use
   atomic database transactions or equivalent concurrency control.
   If simultaneous requests target the last seat, only one succeeds. Refresh available options for the other.
   Prevent conflicting updates to the same guest, including Super Administrator actions.
   Transfers and swaps must validate all affected seats atomically; do not release a valid existing assignment
   until the replacement is secured. Optional short-lived seat holds may reduce conflicts.

06 / OPERATIONS

Reliability and rollout
Prioritize a dependable workflow over elaborate features. Confirm the final seating plan and staffing before
production deployment.


17. Technical and operational requirements
   Mobile and desktop browser compatibility; multi-device simultaneous operation; live synchronization; secure
   transport and storage; role enforcement and audit logs.
   Back up guest and seating records; provide recovery procedures. Test real-world peak load, including
   multiple staff and guest devices registering at once.
   Maintain an offline contingency guest list and seating plan. If connectivity fails, use a coordinated single
   allocation authority or pre-partitioned offline seating ranges; reconcile before resuming online assignments.
   Keep distinct statuses for RSVP, arrival/check-in, reservation/assignment and notification. A preferred
   assignment may exist before arrival; RSVP confirmation is not a prerequisite to authorized check-in.

Core: QR verification, express and assisted flows, exclusive delegation, industry recommendations, group

08 / DECISIONS

Implementation assumptions
The following items distinguish planning assumptions and proposed enhancements from the business rules
expressly confirmed in the discussion.


21. Confirmed business rules
   Preferred guests have preassigned tables and seats; regular guests and employees receive assignments on
   arrival.
   Industry affinity should guide recommendations; no sequential table-filling requirement. The designated
   person selects a suitable table/seat.
   Group arrivals are supported; preferred no-show reservations are released manually.
   Unlisted guests may be accepted through an authorized override. Every table has a hard limit of 10.
   Personnel have designated guests and tables, without overlapping delegation; one Super Administrator may
   assign across all tables.
   Email seating assignments automatically where possible, with QR-based registration preferred; allow
   changes as needed.

22. Decisions to finalize with ICD and developer

  Item                                    Proposed default / question

  Guest identity and QR                   Common reception QR; agree on a secondary identifier or assisted verification and
                                          secure status retrieval.

  Seating eligibility                     Dedicated employee tables with configurable mixed tables; confirm which tables
                                          permit regular guests alongside preferred guests.

  Unlisted / no-show authority            Super Administrator approves unlisted guests; confirm whether reservation release
                                          may be delegated within scope.

  Seat numbers                            Mandatory for preferred guests; optional for others unless physical seats are
                                          numbered.

  Industry categories                     ICD supplies industry classifications and related-sector mappings; decide whether
                                          optional affinity/exclusion tags are needed.

  Final capacity and staffing             300 is a working assumption, not a confirmed count. Reconcile external guests,
                                          employees and actual venue table inventory; test peak processing time and staff
                                          coverage.


Technology stack and hosting are left to the developer. The specification defines required behavior, not a prescribed
implementation. Optional enhancements should not delay the core event-day workflow.
