## Before you start

This guide is for **people who want to work at the school** and do not have an ATLAS account yet. If you already work at the school, apply with your own account instead: see [the employee guide](employee#apply-for-a-job-opening).

- The openings are on the ATLAS home page: choose **Job openings** at the top of the sign-in page, or **See job openings** under the sign-in form. You can read them without signing in.
- To apply, you sign up for an **applicant account** with your name, email, a username, a password and, if you like, your mobile number. The school may also ask a few details about you, such as your date of birth or the campus you would like to work at; the form shows which, and marks the required ones with a red asterisk. Documents are never asked at sign-up: they come with each application.
- An applicant account is **temporary**. It stays open for a set number of days (365 unless the school chose another number). You are told on the bell before it closes; after that you cannot sign in, and a while later the account and the personal details in your applications are removed.
- Your account opens only **Job openings**, **My applications** and your own account (**My profile**, **My security**). It is not an employee account.
- To try things out, use the demo school: sign in as `applicant.demo` (password `demo1234`), Carla Mendiola, who applied to be an Instructor I.

A first tour with the demo school. It takes about five minutes.

### Read the openings

1. Open the ATLAS home page and choose **Job openings** at the top. Each opening shows the position, the office and campus, the salary grade with the monthly salary, the number of vacancies and the date to apply by.
2. Choose an opening to read its **qualification standards**: education, training, experience and eligibility.

1. Choose **Sign up to apply**.

## How to…

### Find the job openings

1. Open the ATLAS home page.
2. Choose **Job openings** at the top (on a phone, the briefcase), or **See job openings** under the sign-in form.

The list shows only openings that are open now and that anyone may apply for. When there are none, the link is not shown.

### Sign up for an applicant account

1. On **Job openings**, choose **Sign up to apply**.
2. Fill in your **Full name** and **Email**. Add your **Mobile number** if you want HR to be able to call you (your school may require it).
3. Fill in any other details the form asks (see the table below). Each is marked required (a red asterisk) or *(optional)*.
4. Choose a username: 3 to 40 lowercase letters, numbers, dots, dashes or underscores.
5. Choose a password: at least 8 characters (your school may ask for more).
6. Read **Your privacy**: it lists exactly what this form keeps.
7. Tick **I have read this notice and agree to it** and choose **Create my applicant account**.

The details your school may ask:

| Field | What to give |
|---|---|
| Sex | Pick **Male** or **Female** |
| Date of birth | Pick the date. Under the box ATLAS shows it as it reads it, for example *Apr 17, 1996*, so you can check the day and month. You must be at least 18. |
| Home address | Up to 300 characters |
| Highest educational attainment | The highest level you have reached, finished or not |
| Preferred campus or place of assignment | The campus you would like to work at |

You are signed in at once. Next time, sign in on the ATLAS home page with your username and password, like everyone else. What you gave besides your name, email and mobile is under **My profile → Given at sign-up**.

![Sign up to apply, with a date of birth and a preferred campus asked](applicant-sign-up-fields.jpg)

> **Note:** Each email and username can have one account. If you already have an ATLAS account (you work or study at the school), ATLAS says so: sign in with that account instead.

### Apply for an opening

1. Open **Job openings** and choose the opening.
2. Read the qualification standards.
3. Choose **Apply**.
4. Type a **Cover note** if you like: why you are applying and what HR should know.
5. Choose **Send application**.

Your application shows as *Received*. HR sees your name, email and mobile number from your account, and your note. You can apply once to each opening.

![Apply for an opening](applicant-apply.jpg)

### Follow your application

1. Open **My applications**. Each application shows the school's recruitment stages, with a tick and a date on each stage it has passed, and the latest note from HR.
2. Choose **See its progress**.
3. Under **Progress**, read the stages: a tick for each stage done, a ring around the one it is at now (*Since* its date), and the ones to come. A stage HR went past without using shows as *Skipped*.
4. Read the box under the stages: what happens at this stage, and what you may be asked to do.
5. Under **Notes from HR**, read what HR wrote to you, oldest first, each with its date and the stage it was at.

Each time HR moves your application or writes to you, you are told on the bell at the top; choose the notice to open the application. Notes come from *HR office*. On a phone, the stages are a list down the page.

![An application's progress on a phone](applicant-my-applications-phone.jpg)

### Know the stages

The stages are set by the school's HR office and may differ from these. Unless the school changed them, they are:

| Stage | What happens |
|---|---|
| Application received | HR has your application and checks that it is complete. You may be asked for a missing document. |
| Initial screening | HR compares your application with the qualification standards of the position: education, training, experience and eligibility. You may be asked to send or bring documents. |
| Assessment (examination or interview) | Qualified applicants are assessed, for example by a written examination, a skills test or an interview. HR tells you the date, time and place, and what to bring. |
| Deliberation | The selection board compares the assessed applicants and recommends whom to appoint. There is nothing you need to do. |
| Final decision | The appointing authority chooses from the applicants the board recommended. |

The application ends with an outcome: *Selected* (HR tells you the next steps), *Not qualified* (it does not meet the qualification standards of the position), *Not selected* (another applicant was chosen), or *Withdrawn* (you withdrew it). Once decided, the outcome ends the tracker, with its date.

### Withdraw an application

1. Open **My applications**.
2. Choose **Withdraw** on the application and confirm.

You can withdraw while the opening is not closed and HR has not decided. You can also withdraw from the application's page (**See its progress**). Until the closing date, you can send it again: choose **Apply again**. Withdrawing does not close your account.

### Know when your account closes

1. Open **Job openings** or **My applications**.
2. Read *Your applicant account is open until …* at the top.

You are told on the bell before that date. Applying again does not move it. If you still need your account after it, ask the school's HR office to extend it before it is removed.

## Screens at a glance

| Screen | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Job openings (home page, signed out) | The openings anyone may apply for, their qualification standards, and **Sign up to apply** |
| Job openings | The openings you may apply for, the date your account closes, and each opening with **Apply** |
| My applications | Your applications, where each is in the recruitment stages, the latest note from HR, **Withdraw** and **Apply again** |
| An application (**See its progress**) | Its progress through the stages with dates, what happens now, the notes from HR, the outcome, **Withdraw** |
| My profile, My security | Your own account: your details, your password, 2-step verification and where you are signed in |

## Questions

**ATLAS says "You already have an applicant account with this email."** You signed up before. Sign in with that username on the ATLAS home page. If you have forgotten the password, ask the school's HR office.

**ATLAS says "This email belongs to an account you already have."** You already have an ATLAS account, for example as an employee or a student. Sign in with it instead; employees apply from **People → Job openings**.

**ATLAS says "Your applicant account closed on …"** Your applicant account stays open for a set number of days. Ask the school's HR office to extend it if you still want to apply. Once it is removed, your details are gone and you can sign up again.

**What does the school keep about me, and for how long?** What the sign-up form asked (your name, email, username and mobile number, and any other details it asked, as **Your privacy** lists them) and your applications (with your cover notes), to consider you for its openings and contact you. Only the school's HR staff see them; other applicants never do. They are removed some days after your account closes (the notice at sign-up says how many), or sooner if you ask HR (Data Privacy Act of 2012).

**Who is "HR office" in my notes?**
The school's HR staff. Notes are signed by the office, not by a person. If you have a question about a note, contact the HR office.

**My application went back a stage. Why?**
HR may move an application back one stage, for example when it was moved by mistake or an examination was moved. If HR wrote you a note, it is under **Notes from HR**; otherwise, ask the HR office.

**Why was a stage skipped?**
HR can move an application past a stage that is not needed for it. The skipped stage shows as *Skipped*.

**ATLAS says "Applicants must be at least 18 years old."** The date of birth you picked makes you younger than 18 today. Check the year, and the day and month shown under the date box.

**ATLAS says "… is required."** The school asks that detail of every applicant. Fill it in, or ask the HR office if you cannot.

**How do I correct a detail I gave at sign-up?** Ask the school's HR office. You can see what you gave under **My profile → Given at sign-up**.

**Can I upload my documents or fill in a PDS here?** Not at sign-up, and not yet with an application. HR tells you what to bring or send, for example in a note on your application.

**Why is there no employee ID or branch on the form?** Employees of the school apply with their own account, not an applicant account, so the form never asks for an employee ID. The campus you would like to work at (the branch) is asked only if the school chose to: it is then **Preferred campus or place of assignment**.

**Why don't I see an opening I heard about?** Some openings are for the school's employees only, and an opening is shown only while it is open, until its closing date.

**Can I sign in with Keycloak?** No. Applicant accounts sign in with the username and password you chose.
