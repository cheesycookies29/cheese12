## Before you start

This guide is for **assessors**: the members of a job opening's selection board (in government, the HRMPSB) whom HR names to rate the applicants. You can be a teacher, a dean, or any staff member; you do not need to work in HR.

- HR names you on an opening's board. You are told on the bell, and **Ratings to do** appears in ATLAS People's sidebar, under **Selection board**.
- You see only the openings you are named on: their applicants' names, applications and documents. Nothing else of recruitment.
- You rate each applicant **on your own**. You do not see the other assessors' scores before you submit yours, and only HR sees everyone's.
- Sign in on the ATLAS home page as usual, and open **People**. To try it with the demo school, sign in as `lmercado.demo` (password `demo1234`), a member of the board for *Instructor I*.

## Walkthrough

1. Open **People → Ratings to do**. *Instructor I* is listed, with how many applicants you still have to rate.
2. Choose **Instructor I**. Each applicant is listed with their cover note, their documents (*Complete* or *Missing 2 required*) and your rating: *Not rated*, *Draft* or *Submitted*.
3. On Teresa Malabanan's row, choose **Continue**: you saved a draft earlier. The criteria of the opening's template are listed, each with its weight.
4. Change a **Score** (0 to 100). **Your total** and each criterion's **Points** change as you type.
5. Choose **Submit rating**. It now counts in the result.
6. On Carla Mendiola's row, choose the number of files under **Documents**. Download her transcript to read it; HR checks the documents, you only read them.

## How to…

### Find your ratings to do

1. Open **People**. If you have an employee record, **My day** says *Your ratings to do* when there are some.
2. Choose **Ratings to do** in the sidebar. The number beside it is the applicants you still have to rate.
3. Choose an opening.

### Rate an applicant

1. In the opening, choose **Rate** on the applicant's row (**Continue** for a draft, **Your rating** for one you submitted).
2. Read the criteria: each has a weight, and a short description of what it rates.
3. Type a **Score** from 0 to 100 for each criterion you have rated, and a **Comment** if you like. Leave a criterion blank until it has been rated, for example an interview still to come.
4. Choose **Save draft** to come back to it later. A draft does not count.
5. Choose **Submit rating** when you are done.

Once submitted, your rating counts in the applicant's result, and you cannot turn it back into a draft. You can still change your scores and choose **Submit changes**: the earlier total is kept, and HR sees the change in the audit log. You cannot rate your own application.

### Rate a performance rating

Some templates have a **Performance rating** criterion, for employees applying for a promotion.

1. Pick the **Adjectival rating** from the list (for example *Very Satisfactory = 90*): the score is filled in. Or leave it on **Typed score** and type the score.
2. Type the **Source**, for example `IPCR Jan–Jun 2026: Very Satisfactory`.

For an applicant from outside the school it usually says *Not applicable (external applicant)*: you give no score, and the other criteria share its weight.

### Read an applicant's documents

1. In the opening, choose the files under **Documents** on the applicant's row (or **Open the checklist** on their rating page).
2. Choose a file to download it.

You can read the documents, not mark them: HR accepts them or asks the applicant for a new file.

|---|---|

| An opening (from Ratings to do) | Its applicants, their cover notes and documents, your rating of each, the position's qualification standards and the board's members |
| Rate an applicant | Your scores and comments per criterion, your total; Save draft and Submit rating |
| Documents of an applicant | The checklist and the files, read-only |

## Questions

**Can I see the other assessors' scores?**
No. Before you submit, you see only your own. After you submit, you may see the combined result (total and rank) if the school allows it; you never see another assessor's scores. HR sees them all.

**How is the result worked out?**
By the rule of the opening's template: the average of the assessors' scores per criterion (the usual rule), the average without the highest and lowest score, or the board's consensus, entered by the secretariat after the board meets. Only submitted ratings count.

**I cannot find an opening I was told about.**
Ask HR to name you on its selection board. You see only the openings you are named on, and HR may also have closed it.

**Does the applicant see my name or my scores?**
No. Applicants never see scores, comments, ranks or who rated them.

**Can I change my rating after submitting?**
Yes: change the scores and choose **Submit changes**. Your earlier total is kept, and the change is in the audit log.
