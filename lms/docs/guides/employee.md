## Before you start

This guide is for **everyone with an employee record**: teachers, registrars, deans, non-teaching staff, and the HR, payroll and Finance officers for their own records.

- Sign in on the ATLAS home page and open the **People** tile. Teachers and registrars use the sign-in they already have; non-teaching staff get one from HR.
- If you see **Getting started** instead of **My day**, your account is not linked to your employee record yet. Ask the HR office to link it (they do it from your record under **Employees → Sign-in account**). A Finance office account is linked by an administrator.
- The **Finance** tile on the ATLAS home page has your accountabilities and your supply and purchase requests. Finance officers find **My accountabilities** at the bottom of the Finance sidebar.
- To try things out, use the demo school: sign in as `staff.demo` (password `demo1234`), Nestor Abello, the college librarian. `teacher.demo` has the same in People, and property in her custody in Finance.

## Walkthrough

### Clock in

1. Sign in as `staff.demo` and open **People**. **My day** opens, with the time, today's status and your schedule.
2. Click **Clock in**. The first time, your browser asks whether ATLAS may use your location; your answer does not stop the clock-in.
3. At the end of the day, click **Clock out**.

![My day: the clock, this pay period, leave and the latest payslip](employee-my-day.jpg)

On a phone, **My day** shows the same clock. It works the same way.

![My day on a phone](employee-my-day-phone.jpg)

### Check your attendance

1. Open **My attendance**. It shows the current pay period: each day's time in and out, hours worked, late, undertime and overtime.
2. Change **From** and **To** to see other days.

![My attendance](employee-attendance.jpg)

### File a leave request

1. Open **Leave & requests**. Your **Leave balances** for this year are at the top: what is left of each leave type, with days used and pending.
2. Click **File a request**.
3. Choose the **Request** (for example *Vacation Leave*) and the dates. Below the dates, ATLAS counts the work days and shows how many days of that leave you have left.
4. Add a reason and click **Submit for approval**.

![Filing a request](employee-file-request.jpg)

### See a payslip

1. Open **My payslips** and click a pay period.
2. Click **Print / Save as PDF** to print it or keep a copy.

![A payslip under My payslips](employee-payslip.jpg)

## How to…

### Clock in and out

1. Open **My day**.
2. Click **Clock in** when you start and **Clock out** when you leave.

Times are the server's, not your device's. Your location is recorded when you clock in; only HR and administrators can see it. If the location cannot be read (you said no, or the address is not secure), you are clocked in without it. Late, undertime and overtime are worked out from your schedule, shown under the clock.

> **Tip:** Forgot to clock out? Your record stays *Open* and that day is not paid until HR fixes it. Tell HR the time you left.

Some offices have a shared **Time clock** computer instead: pick your name and click **Clock in** or **Clock out** there.

### File leave or remote work

1. Open **Leave & requests** and click **File a request** (or **File a request** on **My day**).
2. Under **Request**, choose a leave type, or *Remote work (work from home)* under **Work arrangement**.
3. Pick **From** and **To**. Rest days are not counted.
4. Write a reason. It is optional, but helps your approver.
5. Attach a medical certificate if the leave needs one (see below).
6. Click **Submit for approval**.

The request shows *Pending* under **Leave & requests**. When HR approves it, the days appear in **My attendance**: leave days come off your balance, and a remote-work day counts as a full day. You cannot file more days than you have left, or dates that overlap another pending or approved request. You only see the leave types you are entitled to.

On an approved remote-work day you can still clock in and out to record your hours.

### Attach a medical certificate

A medical certificate is required when the leave type asks for one, for example **Sick Leave of more than 3 work days**. The form says *Medical certificate (required)* when it is.

1. In **File a request**, choose the file under **Medical certificate**: a PDF or a photo, up to 5 MB. Photos are resized automatically.
2. To add or replace it later, open **Leave & requests** and click **Attach certificate** (or **Replace certificate**) on the pending request.

When a certificate is required, the request cannot be submitted without it.

### Withdraw a request

1. Open **Leave & requests**.
2. Click **Withdraw** on the pending request and confirm.

Only pending requests can be withdrawn. To undo an approved one, ask HR to cancel it.

![Leave balances and your requests](employee-requests.jpg)

### Get your payslips

1. Open **My payslips**. Each finalized payroll has a row with gross pay, deductions and net pay. Bonus payslips (mid-year bonus, year-end bonus and cash gift, PEI, clothing allowance) are listed with their name.
2. Click a row to open the payslip.
3. Click **Print / Save as PDF**.

Payslips appear once payroll for the period is finalized. Your latest one is also on **My day**. A payslip shows your earnings, deductions (contributions, tax and loans), net pay, attendance for the period, and what the government or employer pays on top. Report a discrepancy to HR within 5 working days of the pay date.

### Check your profile and change your password

1. Open **My profile** to see your personal details, employment, schedule, government IDs and bank account.
2. Click **Change password** to choose a new password.

To change any of your details, contact HR. HR managers and payroll officers cannot change their own record, leave or payslip: another officer or an administrator does it.

### See your property and cash advances

1. Open the **Finance** tile on the ATLAS home page.
2. **My accountabilities** shows the property in your custody (with its PAR or ICS), your unliquidated cash advances with the date to liquidate by, and official receipts issued to you.

You are accountable for the property until it is returned to the supply office or transferred on a PTR/ITR. If nothing appears and you expect it, your account may not be linked to a card in Finance's card file yet: ask the accounting office.

### Request supplies or a purchase

1. In Finance, open **Supply & purchase requests**.
2. For supplies in stock, click **Request supplies**. Choose your **Office**, write the **Purpose**, pick each item and quantity, and click **Request**. The requisition (RIS) goes to the supply office.
3. For something to be bought, click **Purchase request**. Fill in the office, fund cluster, purpose, your position and the items with their estimated cost, and click **Submit**. The purchase request (PR) goes to the approving officer.
4. Your requisitions and purchase requests are listed with their status. Click one to open it, print it, or **Cancel** it while it is still open.

## Questions

**Why does it say I was absent?**
A scheduled work day with no attendance record counts as an absence. If you worked, ask HR to correct your record.

**Can someone else see where I clocked in?**
Only HR managers and administrators. Your payroll officer and other employees cannot.

**Why can't I choose Maternity or Paternity Leave?**
Some leave types are for female or male employees only, using the gender on your record, and some are given only to selected employees. Ask HR if you think you should have one.

**Why is my vacation leave lower than I expected?**
Under government payroll, tardiness and undertime are charged to vacation leave credits first (CSC rules), when payroll is finalized.

**Why is a loan missing from this payslip?**
A loan is not taken when it would bring your net pay below ₱5,000 a month (GAA). The payslip says so under *Not deducted this time*.

**Why can't I see my payslip yet?**
It appears when the payroll officer finalizes the payroll for that period.
