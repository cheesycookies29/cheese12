## Before you start

This guide is for students. You use three ATLAS systems:

- **Learn** for your classes: lessons, resources, activities, quizzes, attendance, grades, enrollment and your weekly schedule.
- **Academic** for your official record: your evaluation against your curriculum, posted grades, your Certificate of Registration (COR) and document requests.
- **Finance** for the official receipts the cashier issued in your name.

To sign in:

1. Open the ATLAS home page and sign in with the username and password the registrar gave you. If the password was set by someone else, ATLAS asks you to choose a new one first.
2. Under **Your systems**, choose **Learn**, **Academic** or **Finance**.
3. To move to another system later, use the grid button (**All systems**) at the top right. The **?** button next to it opens these guides.

Your name at the top right opens a menu with **Change password** and **Sign out**.

> **Note:** What you see in Learn depends on what your school has switched on. If a menu item in this guide is missing (for example **Enrollment** outside enrollment season), the school has turned it off for now.

## How to…

### Hand in an assignment

1. Open the assignment from **Home → To do**, or from the class's **Activities** tab.
2. Read the **Instructions**.
3. Under **Hand in your work**, write your **Answer**, attach a **File** (up to 10 MB unless your school set another limit; drop it on the box or choose it), or paste a **Link to your work**. One of the three is enough.
4. Press **Hand in**.

Your work then shows under **Your work** as *Handed in*. Until your teacher scores it, you can change it: edit the form under **Hand in again** and press **Replace my work**. Once it is scored, you see the score and your teacher's feedback, and it can no longer be replaced.

> **Note:** After the due date you can hand in only if the assignment says *Late work accepted*; that work is marked *Late*.

### Take a quiz or exam

1. Open the quiz from **Home → To do** or the class's **Activities** tab.
2. Check the time limit and attempts, then press **Start quiz** (or **Start another attempt**).
3. Answer each question. For *Choose all that apply*, tick every right choice; wrong ticks take points away.
4. Press **Submit** and confirm. If some questions are unanswered, ATLAS tells you how many first.

The highest score of your attempts counts (**Counts toward your grade**). Depending on the quiz, the correct answers appear right after you submit, after the quiz closes, or never.

> **Important:** The clock is kept by the school's server, not your computer. It keeps running if you close the page, and when time is up the quiz submits the answers you saved.

### Read lessons and announcements

1. Open the class from **My classes**.
2. **Lessons** lists the lessons in order; **Open material** opens a lesson's linked slides, video or reading.
3. **Announcements** shows what your teacher posted to the class. The newest ones also appear on **Home**.

### Check your attendance

1. Open the class and choose **Attendance**.
2. You see your **Attendance rate**, the number of days *Present*, *Late*, *Absent* and *Excused*, and every day your teacher took attendance.

Late counts as attended; excused days are left out of the rate.

### Check your grades

1. In the sidebar, open **Grades**. It lists every class by term, with your **Running score** and **Final grade** (*Passed* or *Failed*).
2. Click a class to see its **Grades** tab: your running grade, points scored, activities done, and the score of each activity.

The running grade counts scored work only. Your teacher gives the final grade.

### Request a seat in a section

Each section you can take has one of three buttons:

- **Request a seat**: the registrar reviews your request; until then it shows as *Pending approval*.
- **Enroll**: a seat is free and your school does not require approval, so you are enrolled at once.
- **Join waitlist**: the section is full; you join the line and see your place in it.

1. Open **Enrollment**. The notice at the top says how many units you hold, whether the registrar reviews requests, and whether full sections take a waitlist.
2. Find the section. Use the search box to filter by course, teacher or day.
3. Press the button on its row.
4. If the section shows *Needs an enrollment key*, type the **Enrollment key** your teacher or the registrar gave you.
5. Confirm with the same button in the window.

A message tells you the result, and the row now shows your status. In the demo, *IT220 A* needs the key `DB2026`.

### Follow, withdraw or drop a request

1. Open **Enrollment → My enrollment**. It lists your requests and classes for the term with their status, your place on a waitlist, and the last update.
2. To cancel a pending or waitlisted request, press **Withdraw**. To leave a class you are enrolled in, press **Drop**, then **Drop class**.

Your seat goes to the first student on the waitlist. You can drop an enrolled class until the term's drop deadline; after that, ask the registrar.

### See your week

Open **Schedule** for the week's timetable. Pending requests are drawn dashed. If the school has more than one term, pick one at the top.

### Use the calendar

**Calendar** puts everything with a date in one place: your class meetings, what is due (and whether you handed it in), examinations, holidays and days without classes, school events and the term's dates (enrollment, the last day to drop, the first and last day of classes).

1. Open **Calendar**. **Month** shows the month; **Week** lists each day; **List** shows only the days with something on them (the view on a phone).
2. Use **‹** and **›** to move, and **Today** to come back.
3. Choose an entry for its details: the time, the room, the class, and **Open the class** or **Open the activity**. Choose a day (or **2 more**) to see everything on it.
4. The coloured tick boxes above the calendar (**Classes**, **Due dates**, **Examinations**, …) show or hide each kind; the calendar remembers your choice.

Class meetings do not appear on holidays and days without classes. Work you handed in is greyed out with a check mark; a red **!** marks missing work. Events that last several days run as one bar across the days.

![The calendar: classes, due dates, exams and holidays](student-calendar.jpg)

To see the calendar in Google Calendar, Outlook or your phone, choose **Subscribe → Make my calendar address**, then **Copy address** and add it to your calendar app (the dialog says where). Your calendar app reads the calendar's own address, which keeps it up to date every few hours. Anyone with the address sees your calendar: if you shared it by mistake, choose **Get a new address** (the old one stops working) or **Stop sharing**.

With the keyboard: Tab to the month, move between days with the arrow keys (Home and End for the start and end of the week, Page Up and Page Down for the month before and after), and press Enter for the day's list.

### React to school news

1. Open **News & events**.
2. Press **Like**, **Love** or **Celebrate** under a post, or write a comment and press **Post**.
3. To remove your comment, press the bin next to it.

### See who follows your records

Open **Parents & guardians**. It lists each parent or guardian the registrar's office linked to your records, what each can see (for example *Grades* and *Attendance*), and when they last looked. They can look but not change anything. To change who sees what, contact the registrar's office.

### Print your Certificate of Registration (COR)

1. Open **Academic → Registration (COR)**. It lists this term's subjects and units.
2. Press **Print my COR**. The button appears once the registrar has enrolled you in at least one subject.
3. On the certificate, press **Print**.

The COR carries a code and a QR code; anyone can scan it to check that it is genuine.

### Request an official document

1. Open **Academic → Document requests** and press **Request a document**.
2. Choose the **Document** (for example *Transcript of Records* or *Certificate of Grades*; the fee per copy is shown), the number of **Copies** and the **Purpose**.
3. Press **Send request**. Note its reference number.
4. Pay at the cashier with that reference number.

The status then moves from *Requested* to *Paid*, *Processing* and *Ready for release*: bring your ID to the registrar to pick it up. While a request is still *Requested*, you can **Withdraw** it.

### Find a receipt

Open **Finance → My payments** and press **View** next to the O.R. number to see or print the receipt.

## Questions

**Why can't I take a section?**
Tick **Show sections I can't take, and why** under **Enrollment**: the reason is on the row. A request is checked in this order: the section and the term's enrollment window are open; the enrollment key matches; you do not already hold a place in the same course this term; you have not already passed it; you have passed every prerequisite (a final grade of at least the passing grade, 75 by default); you stay within the unit limit (24 by default, counting enrolled, pending and waitlisted units); and it does not clash with your other classes. Back-to-back classes are fine.

**How does the waitlist work?**
When a seat frees up (someone drops, a request is rejected, or the registrar adds seats), the first student in line moves up automatically. Your place shows under **My enrollment**.

**My grade in Learn is not in Academic yet. Why?**
Learn shows your teacher's gradebook. A grade reaches your permanent record in Academic only after your teacher submits the grade sheet, the dean approves it and the registrar posts it. Academic's **Grades** shows posted grades only.

**What do the grades in Academic mean?**
Final grades in Learn are percentages. Academic turns them into the grading scale (for example 99–100 is 1.00, 75 is 3.00, below 75 is 5.00). INC means incomplete.

**I lost my place in a quiz. Did I lose my answers?**
No. Answers save as you go. Open the quiz again to carry on while time is left; when time runs out, what you saved is submitted.

**Why can't I see the correct answers of a quiz?**
Your teacher chooses when they appear: right after you submit, after the quiz closes, or never (score only). Until then the answer key is never sent to your computer.

**A payment I made is not under My payments.**
Only official receipts issued in your name appear. Ask the cashier.
