## Before you start

This guide is for deans and program chairs. Your work is in **ATLAS Academic**:

- approving the grade sheets teachers submit, or returning them with a reason;
- approving or rejecting grade changes: corrections of posted grades and INC completions;
- building each program's curriculum and posting it;
- looking up students' records, evaluations and reports.

Sign in on the ATLAS home page with your username and password, then open the **Academic** tile (*Open Academic*). If you work at more than one campus, choose your name at the top right and pick the campus under **Switch campus**; every list follows the campus you are on.

You see every class and student at your campus, not only your college's. Student records are read-only for you: the registrar's office edits them, posts grades and issues documents (see [the registrar's guide](registrar)).

## How to…

### Approve or return a grade sheet

1. Open **Grade sheets**. Choose **Show: Waiting for approval** (or choose the item on the dashboard). Sheets waiting for you are listed from every term.
2. Open a sheet. Check each student's **Final (%)**, **Grade** and **Remark**. Choose **Print** for a paper copy with signature lines.
3. Choose **Approve**. Or choose **Return**, write **What should be fixed?**, and choose **Return to the teacher**.

The result: an approved sheet goes to the registrar, who posts it to the students' permanent records. A returned sheet goes back to the teacher with your reason; it shows as *Returned to the teacher* until they submit it again. The **Trail** at the bottom of each sheet lists who submitted, approved, returned and posted it, and when.

### Decide a grade change or an INC completion

1. Open **Grade changes**. The **Waiting** tab lists each change: the student, the subject, *INC completion* or *Rectification*, the grade from and to, the reason, and who asked.
2. Choose the student's name to see their grades first, if you need to.
3. Choose **Approve** or **Reject**.

The result: an approved change updates the student's record; the old grade stays in the record's history. A rejected change leaves the record as it was. The **Approved**, **Rejected** and **All** tabs keep the past decisions.

### Create a curriculum

1. Open **Curricula** and choose **New curriculum**.
2. Choose the **Program**. Type the **Series**, the year it takes effect (for example *2027*). **Code** defaults to the program's code. Add a **Major** if the program has one, and the number of **Years**.
3. Under **Start from**, choose *Empty terms*, or a copy of an existing curriculum.
4. Choose **Create**. The curriculum opens as a *Draft*.

### Build and post a draft curriculum

1. Open the draft under **Curricula**.
2. In each term, add subjects with **+ Add a subject…**; the program's own courses are listed first. Remove a subject with the **×** on its row.
3. To add a term, use **Add a term** at the bottom: choose the year level and *First Semester*, *Second Semester* or *Summer*, and choose **Add**. An empty term can be removed with its **×**.
4. Set the **Lab** units of each subject where needed. They are used for the FHE billing report.
5. Read the warnings under **Check the order of prerequisites**: a prerequisite that is not in the curriculum, or that comes in the same or a later term.
6. Choose **Post** and confirm.

The result: the curriculum is posted and locked, so students' evaluations stay stable. **Print prospectus** prints it.

> **Note:** The **Lab** units belong to the course, not to one curriculum. Changing them changes the course everywhere it is used.

### Change a posted curriculum

A posted curriculum cannot be edited.

1. Open it and choose **New series from this**.
2. Type the **New series** and choose **Create draft**.
3. Make your changes in the new draft and post it.

To stop a curriculum from being assigned to new students, choose **Retire**. Students already on it keep it. A draft that is no longer needed can be removed with **Delete draft**.

### Look up a student or a report

1. Open **Students**. Search by name, student number or email, or filter by program or year level, or change **All students** to *No curriculum*, *Missing requirements* or *With an INC*.
2. Open a student. **Evaluation** shows their record against their curriculum: subjects passed, credited, being taken, those they can take next, and back subjects. **Print evaluation** prints it.
3. Open **Reports** for the enrollment list and summary, failing grades and INCs, the dean's list and candidates for graduation. Each prints on A4 and exports to CSV.

### Manage colleges

1. Open **Settings**. The grading scale, rules and requirements are shown for reference; only the registrar and administrators change them.
2. Under **Colleges**, choose **Add college**, or **Edit** on a college. Fill in the **Code**, **Name** and **Dean**, and tick the **Programs** that belong to it.
3. Choose **Add** (or **Save**).

## Screens at a glance

| **Grade sheets** | Every class's grade sheet; approve or return submitted ones |
| **Grade changes** | Rectifications and INC completions to approve or reject |
| **Document requests** | Students' document requests and their status (view only) |
| **Curricula** | Build, post, copy and retire each program's curriculum |
| **Reports** | Enrollment list, summary, failing and INC, dean's list, graduation |
| **Settings** | The academic rules (view only) and the colleges, which you manage |

## Questions

**Can I approve a grade change I asked for myself?** No. A grade change must be approved by someone other than the person who asked for it.

**I approved a sheet by mistake. Can I undo it?** Not from your screen. Before the registrar posts it, an administrator can return it to the teacher. After posting, corrections are grade changes on each student's record.

**Why do I see classes from other colleges?** Grade sheets and students are listed by campus, not by college.

**Why can't I edit a student's record or print a document?** These are the registrar's office's work. You can read every tab of the record and print the evaluation.

**Can I post a curriculum with prerequisite warnings?** Yes, the confirmation tells you how many warnings there are. It is better to fix them first: a student's evaluation shows a subject as *Can take* only when its prerequisites are passed.

**Where do the dean's list rules come from?** From **Settings → Rules and signatory**, set by the registrar: the highest GWA allowed, the lowest grade allowed, no INC, dropped or failed subject, and the least number of units.
