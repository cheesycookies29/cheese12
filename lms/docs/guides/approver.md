## Before you start

This guide is for the head of agency (the University or College President) or the official authorized to approve in their place. In **ATLAS Finance** you sign three things:

- **Purchase requests** (PR) from the school's offices, before anything is bought.
- **Purchase orders** (PO), once the budget office has obligated their funds.
- **Disbursement vouchers** (DV), **Box D**: *approved for payment*, after the Chief Accountant has certified Box C.

Everything else in Finance you can open and read, so you can check a document before you approve it. You cannot prepare, certify or pay anything, and you cannot approve a voucher you prepared or certified yourself, or a purchase request you filed.

**Signing in.** Sign in on the ATLAS home page with your Finance account and open **Finance**. Only an administrator creates or changes Finance accounts.

> **Tip:** The **?** button at the top of every page opens these guides. The other offices have theirs: [Chief Accountant](accountant), [Budget Officer](budget-officer), [Cashier](cashier), [Supply Officer](supply-officer).

## Walkthrough

Try this with the demo school: sign in as `head.demo` (password `demo1234`).

1. Open **Finance**. The **Dashboard** opens. Under **Waiting for you** are *1 voucher to approve (Box D)* and *1 purchase request to approve*. The cards at the top show the cash in all funds, how much of the year's allotments is obligated, how much of the obligations is paid, and collections against the target.

![The approving officer's dashboard](approver-dashboard.jpg)

2. Choose **1 voucher to approve (Box D)**. **Disbursement vouchers** opens on **For approval (Box D)**. Open the Agri-Pinoy Farm Supply voucher.
3. Read it before signing. In the boxes across the top, Box A (the requesting office) and Box C (the accountant) are done; Box D is yours. Choose the **Obligation** number to see the ORS and what is left on it. **Delivery** is the IAR that accepted the supplies, and **Amount** shows the gross, the taxes withheld and the net the supplier will receive.

![A voucher waiting for Box D](approver-dv-approve.jpg)

4. Choose **Approve (Box D)**. The dialog repeats the net amount and the payee. Choose **Approve for payment**. The voucher goes to the cashier's **To pay** list.
5. Go back to the **Dashboard** and choose **1 purchase request to approve**. Open the Registrar's request for security paper and toner. Check the purpose, the items and the estimated cost.

![A purchase request waiting for approval](approver-pr.jpg)

6. Choose **Approve**. The request goes to the supply office, which prepares the purchase order.
7. Open **Find a document**, type `Agri` and choose **Find**. Every document for that supplier is listed: the voucher, the ORS, the purchase order and the supplier's card. Choose a number to open it.

![Finding a supplier's documents](approver-find.jpg)

## How to…

### Approve a voucher (Box D)

1. Open **Disbursement vouchers**. Your list opens on **For approval (Box D)**.
2. Open the voucher and check the payee, the obligation, the particulars and the net amount.
3. Choose **Approve (Box D)** and then **Approve for payment**.

The voucher is ready for the cashier to pay by check or on an LDDAP-ADA. If something is wrong, do not approve it: ask the cashier or the Chief Accountant to correct or cancel it. Only they can cancel a voucher.

### Approve a purchase request

1. Open **Purchase requests**, then the **For approval** tab.
2. Open the request and check the office, purpose, items and estimate.
3. Choose **Approve**.

To turn a request down, choose **Cancel**, give the reason and choose **Cancel request**. The requester sees it as cancelled.

### Approve a purchase order

1. Open **Purchase orders** and open the order (drafts are on the **Drafts** tab).
2. Check the supplier, the mode of procurement, the items and the **Obligation** line: it must say *obligated*.
3. Choose **Approve PO**.

The button appears only once the PO's ORS/BURS is obligated by the budget office. After approval, the supply office serves the order to the supplier and accepts the deliveries.

### Approve a payroll

Government payroll from ATLAS People reaches you as a voucher like any other, in **For approval (Box D)**, after the budget office has obligated it and the accountant has certified it. Its particulars start with *Payment of* the payroll run and give the net pay credited to the employees' accounts. Approve it the same way. The remittance vouchers to GSIS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG and BIR that follow come to you too.

### Check a document before approving

1. Open **Find a document**.
2. Type the number from the paper form (DV, JEV, ORS/BURS, PR, PO, IAR, RIS, O.R., RCD, LDDAP-ADA, property or PAR/ICS number), a check number, or part of a payee's or supplier's name, and choose **Find**.
3. Choose the number to open it.

From a voucher you can open its payee's card (**Card file**) to see everything paid to them, the taxes withheld and any property in their custody.

### Read the school's finances

- **Budget reports**: the SAAODB (FAR 1) by program and class, the RAOD, the aging of unpaid obligations (FAR 3) and the SCBAA.
- **Trial balance** and **Financial statements**: per fund cluster or all funds. The statements print with your signature line under *Approved*.
- **Revenue monitoring**: the school's own collections by month against the targets.
- **Audit trail**: who did what in Finance, newest first.

## Questions

**Why is there no Approve button on this voucher?** Only a voucher certified by the accountant (Box C) can be approved. A draft is still with the cashier or the accountant.

**ATLAS says I must be someone other than the preparer or certifier.** Separation of duties: Box D is signed by someone who did not prepare or certify the voucher. Ask another authorized official to approve it.

**Why is there no Approve PO button?** The PO's obligation request is not yet obligated. The budget office certifies Box B first.

**Can I approve my own purchase request?** No. A request is never approved by the person who filed it.

**Can I undo an approval?** No. If an approved voucher should not be paid, the cashier or the accountant cancels it with the reason; its charges go back to the obligation.

**Who can give me Finance access, or take it away?** Only an administrator creates or changes Finance accounts.
