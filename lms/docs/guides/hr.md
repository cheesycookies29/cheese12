## Before you start

This guide is for **HR managers**: the people who keep employee records and the plantilla, watch attendance, approve leave, give employees their sign-in accounts and post job openings. Payroll is prepared by the payroll officer (see [the payroll officer's guide](payroll)); you can view it and change payroll setup. Your own time clock, leave and payslips are in [the employee guide](employee).

- Sign in on the ATLAS home page and open the **People** tile. You land on the **HR dashboard**.
- Everything you see is for **one campus at a time**: the one named under your name at the top right. If you work at more than one campus, switch from that menu (see [Switch campus](#switch-campus)).
- HR settings, payroll setup and leave types are shared by every campus. Employees, attendance, requests, clock-in locations and payroll runs belong to a campus.
- To try things out, use the demo school: sign in as `hr.demo` (password `demo1234`). Josefina Dimaculangan is HR manager for Main Campus and Sipocot Campus.

## How to…

### Add an employee

1. Open **Employees** and choose **Add employee** (also on the HR dashboard).
2. If the person already signs in to Learn (a teacher, registrar or dean), pick their account under **Already has an account?**. ATLAS fills in the name, email and department and links the account when you save.
3. Fill in **Personal information**. Set **Gender**: maternity and paternity leave use it.
4. Under **Employment**, enter the **Employee no.** (the next free number is suggested), **Category** (teaching or non-teaching), position, office, **Appointment status**, **Date hired** and, if you have more than one campus, the **Campus**. Pick the **Plantilla item** from the vacant items in the register, or leave it at *None* (job orders and contracts of service are not on the plantilla).
5. Under **Compensation**, pick the **Salary grade** and **Step**. The monthly salary from the salary schedule is shown beside them. For someone without a plantilla position (a job order paid by the day), leave the grade at *None (fixed rate)* and enter the **Pay type** and **Rate**. Tick **PERA** and enter RATA only for officials who receive it.
6. Tick **Flexible time (FLEX)** for someone whose clock-in and clock-out times should not affect pay.
7. Check the **Work schedule** (it starts as the default from HR settings), enter the government IDs and bank account, and choose **Create employee**.

The record opens. The employee is included in the next payroll run at that campus.

### Give an employee a sign-in account

1. Open the employee's record under **Employees**.
2. For a teacher, registrar or dean: in the **Sign-in account** card, choose **Link an existing account**, pick their Learn account and choose **Link**.
3. For non-teaching staff: choose **Create a sign-in account**, choose the **Account type** (usually *Staff: self-service only*), check the username, type a temporary password of at least 8 characters and choose **Create account**.

Give the person the username and temporary password. They sign in on the ATLAS home page and are asked to choose their own password. From then on they have self-service in People.

> **Note:** The same list of account types also creates HR managers and payroll officers. Accounts for the Finance offices (Chief Accountant, Budget Officer, Cashier, Supply & Property Officer, Approving Officer) are created, linked and changed only by an administrator, so that nobody can give themselves the power to approve payments.

> **Note:** You cannot change your own attendance, leave credits, leave adjustments, leave days, which leave you get, pay or HR details, and a payroll officer cannot adjust their own payslip. ATLAS answers *You cannot … on your own record. Ask another officer or an administrator.*

### Reset a password, disable or unlink an account

1. Open the employee's record.
2. In **Sign-in account**, choose **Reset password** to set a new temporary password (they are signed out everywhere), or **Change type or status** to change the account type or set it to *Disabled: cannot sign in*.
3. Choose **Unlink** to separate the account from the record. The account stays, but loses self-service until it is linked again. A Finance office account is unlinked by an administrator.

Reset password and Change type or status appear only for People accounts. A teacher's, registrar's or dean's password and status are managed in the Learn admin panel. A Finance office account shows *Only an administrator changes Finance office accounts.* instead. You cannot change your own account type or disable your own account.

### Edit, deactivate or move an employee

1. Open the record and choose **Edit**.
2. To stop paying someone, set **Record status** to *Inactive* and, if they left, the **Date separated**. Inactive employees are left out of new payroll runs. Someone separated after a bonus's cut-off date still gets that bonus.
3. To move someone to another of your campuses, change **Campus**. This is refused while they are in a draft payroll run at the old campus.
4. Choose **Save changes**.

**Delete employee** (at the bottom of the record) removes the record with its attendance and leave. Payslips in past payroll runs are kept. Setting the record to *Inactive* is usually the better choice.

### Record a plantilla item

Record each position as DBM approved it. ATLAS never makes up an item number.

1. Open **Plantilla** and choose **Record an item**.
2. Type the **Item number** exactly as DBM issued it, and the **Position title**. Each item number can be used once.
3. Pick the **Salary grade**. The **Authorized annual salary** shows beside it: the grade's step 1 monthly salary, from the salary schedule, × 12.
4. Pick the **Campus** (when you work at more than one) and the **Office** from Finance's list of offices, or type it when it is not there. Add the **Division** if you like.
5. Under **Fund source and PSIPOP codes**, pick the **Fund cluster** and the **Program / activity / project**, and type the **Area code**, **Area type** and **Level** as they are on the PSIPOP.
6. Leave **Status** at **Vacant**, or pick **Unfunded**. Fill in the **Date created** and the **Authority or reference** the item was created under.
7. Only if the approved item says another amount: type it under **Authorized annual salary** with the **Reason**.
8. Choose **Record item**.

The item is in the register, and its change history starts. Every change after this is in the audit log, with the value before and after.

### Link or unlink an incumbent

1. Open **Plantilla** and choose the vacant item.
2. Choose **Link an incumbent**, pick the employee and choose **Link**. The item becomes **Filled**.

The list has the active employees at your campuses who hold no other item. Job orders and contracts of service are not on the plantilla, so they are never listed. To move someone to another item (a promotion), unlink them from the first one, then link them to the new one.

To unlink, open the item and choose **Unlink**, then confirm. The item becomes **Vacant**.

You can also pick the item on the employee record: **Employees →** the person **→ Edit**, then **Plantilla item**. The list has the vacant items and the one they hold.

> **Important:** Linking an item never changes the employee's status of appointment. A temporary, casual or contractual employee on a plantilla item stays as their record says; change the appointment on the record when a new appointment is issued.

### Post a job opening for a vacant item

1. Open **Plantilla** and choose the vacant item.
2. Choose **Post a job opening**. The opening form opens with the item number, position title, salary grade, campus and office filled in.
3. Fill in the rest as in [Post a job opening](#post-a-job-opening) and choose **Save opening**.

The opening keeps the item it came from, and the item lists its openings. Marking an applicant **Selected** changes nothing on the item: link the person to it when the appointment is made.

### Mark an item unfunded or abolished

1. Open the item and choose **Edit**.
2. Pick **Unfunded** or **Abolished** under **Status**. For an abolished item, type why under **Why it is abolished**.
3. Choose **Save changes**.

An item with an incumbent must be unlinked first. An abolished item is kept for its history but left out of the register's list, its totals and the PSIPOP: pick **Abolished** or **All, with abolished** under the status filter to see it. To bring it back, set it to **Vacant**.

### Review items recorded from employee records

Before the register, the item number was typed on each employee record. ATLAS moved those numbers into the register by itself: a number that matched an item was linked to it; a number not in the register was recorded as a draft item marked **To review**, with the employee linked to it. Nothing typed was lost.

1. Open **Plantilla**. A notice says how many items are to review; choose **Show them**.
2. Choose an item. The notice on it says where it came from, for example a number typed on two records.
3. Choose **Edit**, check the item number against DBM's list, and fill in what is missing (office, fund source, date created). Choose **Save changes**.
4. Choose **Mark as reviewed**.

A draft with no incumbent whose number is not on DBM's list can be removed: **Edit → Remove draft**.

The form has the school's name and seal (from **Branding**), the as-of date, the rows by office, the totals, *Nothing follows*, and three signature lines. The table headings repeat on every page. To set the names on the signature lines, choose **Signatories** on the register: leave the Budget Officer and the head of agency blank to use the names in Finance's signatories.

### Correct attendance

1. Open **Attendance (DTR)**. Pick an employee and dates with **From** and **To** if you need to narrow it down.
2. Choose **Edit** on a row, or **Add record** for a day with no record.
3. Choose the **Type**: *Work (on site)*, *Remote work*, or a leave type. For work, enter **Time in** and **Time out**. Leave **Time out** blank if the person is still working; for a full remote day leave both times blank.
4. Add a note saying why, and choose **Save**.

A record with a time in but no time out shows *Open* and is not paid until it is fixed. An employee can have only one record per day. A leave day always has a leave type, so it comes off that leave's credits. You cannot add or change your own records: another HR manager or an administrator does it.

### Approve overtime

1. Open **Attendance (DTR)**.
2. In the **Overtime** column, choose **Approve** next to the minutes. Or choose **Edit** and tick **Overtime / rest-day work approved**.

Only approved overtime counts in payroll. Under government payroll it is earned as compensatory time-off unless payroll setup pays it.

### Set the campus location for clock-ins

1. Open **Clock-in locations** and choose **Set campus location** (or **Change**).
2. Stand on campus and choose **Use this device's current location**, or paste the latitude and longitude from Google Maps.
3. Set the **Radius (metres)**. 300 m covers most campuses. Set **Keep locations for (days)** (365 by default).
4. Choose **Save**.

Clock-ins farther than the radius are flagged *Away from campus*. They are never blocked. Each campus has its own location. Leave the coordinates blank to record locations without checking them.

### Check where someone clocked in

1. Open **Clock-in locations**. Pick the dates, an employee, and what to show: *All clock-ins*, *Away from campus*, *No location* or *On campus*.
2. Choose **View map** on a row for the map, the accuracy and the distance from campus, or **Details** when no location was recorded (for example *Location permission denied*).
3. Choose **Export CSV** for a file with the coordinates.

Only HR managers and administrators see locations. Approved remote-work days are shown as *Remote day*, not flagged.

### Set up the shared time clock

1. On the computer employees will use, sign in as an HR manager and open **Time clock**.
2. Leave it open. Each employee picks their name under **Employee** and chooses **Clock in** or **Clock out**. The list clears for the next person.

The times are the server's, not the computer's. The **Today** list shows who has clocked in and refreshes every minute.

### Approve, reject or cancel a leave request

1. Open **Leave & approvals → Requests**. It shows *Pending* requests first; change the filter to see approved, rejected, cancelled or all.
2. Choose **Approve** or **Reject** on a row. Add a note to the employee if you like and confirm.
3. To undo an approved request, filter by *Approved*, choose **Cancel** on its row, then **Cancel request**. The leave or remote days are taken out of the DTR and the credits come back.

Approving writes each work day of the request into the DTR: leave days come off the balance; remote-work days count as full days. A request cannot be approved when:

- it is your own request (another approver must do it);
- the employee already has attendance on one of the days;
- the leave type needs a medical certificate and none is attached (the employee attaches it under **Leave & requests**);
- there are not enough credits left.

### Change one employee's leave

1. Open **Leave & approvals → Employee leave**, or choose **Leave** on the employee's record.
2. Pick the **Employee** and **Year**.
3. To give or take away a leave type for this person, use the **Gets it?** switch on its row. *use default* puts it back to the type's rule.
4. To set this person's yearly credits (for example a prorated new hire), choose the **Credits** number.
5. To add or take away days with a reason, such as a carry-over, choose **Adjust** (or **Adjust balance**). Each adjustment is kept with your name.
6. To add leave taken outside the system (filed on paper) to the DTR, choose **Record** (or **Record leave**).
7. To undo, choose **Remove** in the **Adjustments** or **Leave taken** list below the table.

Remaining = credits + adjustments − used − pending. Days from an approved request can only be removed by cancelling the request. If payroll for a day is already finalized, ATLAS warns you that the payslip does not change.

### Manage leave types

1. Open **Leave & approvals → Leave types** and choose **Add leave type**, or **Edit** on a row.
2. Set the **Code**, **Name**, **Annual credits** (blank means no limit), whether it is **Paid leave**, **Who gets it** (everyone, or only employees you add under Employee leave), **Limited to** a gender, and **Medical certificate needed when longer than (days)**.
3. Choose **Add** or **Save**.

A leave type that has been used cannot be deleted; untick **Active** instead. Leave types are the same at every campus.

### Post a job opening

1. Open **Recruitment → Job openings** and choose **New opening**.
2. Type the **Position title** and the **Plantilla item no.** as DBM issued it.
3. Pick the **Salary grade**. The monthly salary (step 1, from the salary schedule in Payroll setup) shows under it, and applicants see it.
4. Fill in the place of assignment: the **Campus** (when you work at more than one) and the **Office**. Set the **Number of vacancies** and, if you like, a **Description**.
5. Under **Qualification standards**, fill in **Education**, **Training**, **Experience** and **Eligibility** as the CSC qualification standards for the position say.
6. Under **Assessment**, pick the **Position category**, for example **Faculty** or **Non-teaching**. The **Assessment template** of that category is filled in; pick another if this opening needs it.
7. Under **Documents to upload**, check the documents applicants are asked for. The list comes from the position category (**HR settings → Applicant documents**): change a name or the **Short note for the applicant**, tick or untick **Required**, choose **Add a document**, or use the arrows and the bin. **Use the category's list** starts again from the category's. Tick **Required documents must be uploaded before applying** only if applicants may not apply without them (off by default).
8. Pick the **Closing date**. Applications are taken through that day.
9. Under **Who may apply**, choose **Open to all** (job applicants and employees) or **Internal only** (employees only, from People).
10. Set **Status** to **Draft** to keep working on it, or **Open** to publish it. Choose **Save opening**.

For a vacant plantilla item, start from the item instead: **Plantilla →** the item **→ Post a job opening** fills in the item number, title, salary grade, campus and office (see [Post a job opening for a vacant item](#post-a-job-opening-for-a-vacant-item)).

An open opening shows on the ATLAS home page (when it is Open to all) and under **Job openings** in every employee's People, until its closing date. To stop applications early, **Edit** it and set **Status** to **Closed**. An opening with no applications can be deleted from **Edit**; one with applications can only be closed, so they are kept.

### Move an application to another stage

Every application goes through the school's **recruitment stages** (see [Set the recruitment stages](#set-the-recruitment-stages)). A new one starts at the first stage, *Application received*.

1. Open **Recruitment → Job openings** and choose the opening.
2. On the applicant's row, choose **Move**. The window says which stage the application is at.
3. Under **Move to**, pick a stage under **Next stages** (any later stage; you can skip one), or an outcome under **Final outcome**: **Selected**, **Not qualified** or **Not selected**.
4. Type a **Note to the applicant** if you like, for example the date and place of an examination.
5. Type an **Internal remark** if you like. Only HR sees it.
6. Choose **Save and tell the applicant**.

The applicant sees the new stage, its date and your note on their application, from *HR office* (never your name), and is told on the bell with a link to it. Each move is kept in the application's **History** and in the audit log (**People**, *Moved a job application to another stage*), with the stage before and after. To write to the applicant without moving the application, pick **Stay at …** and type the note. You cannot move your own application, nor one the applicant withdrew; only the applicant can withdraw.

![Move an application, with a note to the applicant and an internal remark](hr-change-status.jpg)

### Move an application back a stage

1. On the applicant's row, choose **Move**.
2. Under **Back one stage**, pick **Back to …**.
3. Type the **Reason for moving it back**, for example *Moved by mistake: the interview has not been held yet.* It is required.
4. Choose **Save and tell the applicant**.

An application goes back one stage at a time. A decided one (Selected, Not qualified or Not selected) can go back only to the stage it was at before the decision. The reason is kept in the history and the audit log (*Moved a job application back a stage*); the applicant does not see it, only the stage it is at now and any note you add.

### Move several applications at once

1. Open the opening. To see only the applications at one stage, choose that stage under **Applications by stage**.
2. Tick the applications to move, or tick the box in the heading for all of them shown.
3. Choose **Move chosen…**.
4. Pick the stage or final outcome, type one **Note to the applicants** if you like (each reads the same note) and an **Internal remark**.
5. Choose **Move and tell the applicants**.

Each must be at an earlier stage. If one of them cannot move (it is already at that stage, or past it), nothing moves and ATLAS says which and why: untick it and try again. Moving back is one application at a time, each with its reason.

### See an application's history

1. On the applicant's row, choose **History**.

Each move shows its stage, the date and time, who moved it (or *Applicant* for their own steps), the note to the applicant, the internal remark and, for a move back, the reason. Applications sent before the recruitment stages came keep their earlier history, carried over to the stages.

### Set the recruitment stages

1. Open **HR settings** and go down to **Recruitment stages**.
2. Change a stage's **Label**, or **What the applicant reads**: what happens at that stage, and what the applicant may be asked to do.
3. To add a stage, choose **Add a stage** and fill it in. Use the arrows to move a stage up or down.
4. To stop using a stage, tick **Retired**. A stage no application has used yet can also be removed with **Remove**.
5. Under **Final outcomes**, change their labels and what the applicant reads if you like. To stop offering *Not qualified*, tick **Not offered**.
6. Choose **Save recruitment stages**.

The stages are the same at every campus and for every opening. A stage an application has been at cannot be removed, only retired, so every history keeps its meaning: a retired stage stays in the histories, takes no new moves, and is left out of the tracker of applications that never reached it. The final outcomes are fixed (*Selected*, *Not qualified*, *Not selected*, *Withdrawn*): their meaning never changes, only their labels and descriptions. *Withdrawn* is set only by the applicant.

### Choose what the applicant sign-up asks

People outside the school sign up before they apply (see [the applicant's guide](applicant#sign-up-for-an-applicant-account)). **Full name**, **Email**, **Username** and **Password** are always asked and required. You choose the rest.

1. Open **HR settings** and go down to **Applicant sign-up fields**.
2. For each field, choose **Hidden** (not asked), **Optional** (asked, may be left blank) or **Required** (see the table below).
3. Choose **Save HR settings**.

| Field | Default | What applicants pick or type |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile number | Optional | A mobile number |
| Sex | Hidden | Male or Female, as on the Personal Data Sheet |
| Date of birth | Hidden | A date: a real date in the past, and the applicant at least 18 |
| Home address | Hidden | Up to 300 characters |
| Highest educational attainment | Hidden | Elementary, Secondary, Vocational or trade course, College or Graduate studies |
| Preferred campus or place of assignment | Hidden | One of the school's campuses (the "branch") |

The sign-up form shows the Optional and Required fields, marks the required ones with a red asterisk, and its privacy notice lists exactly what it collects. ATLAS checks the same rules when the form is sent: a Hidden field is ignored even if sent, and a Required one that is missing is refused.

Ask only what you need (Data Privacy Act of 2012): by default only the mobile number is asked. Document uploads are never asked at sign-up, because documents come with each application; nor is an employee ID, because employees apply with their own account.

What an applicant gave shows under their name on each application, in **Applicant accounts** and in the CSV export, dates in ATLAS's usual form (*Apr 17, 1996*). Hiding a field later deletes nothing already given. The details go with the account when it is removed. Each change is in the Settings center's **Change history**.

![HR settings → Applicant sign-up fields](hr-signup-fields.jpg)

### Set up the assessment templates

Applicants are rated with an **assessment template**: a list of criteria, each with a weight. Each **position category** (for example Faculty and Non-teaching) has a default template, because the positions have different criteria. ATLAS comes with three templates, *Faculty (example)*, *Non-teaching (example)* and *Promotion, non-teaching (example)*: they are examples, to replace with the criteria and weights of the school's merit selection plan. They rate education, experience, training and eligibility (EETE); the non-teaching one adds a competency examination, an interview and a work sample, the faculty one a teaching demonstration and an interview, and the promotion one a performance rating, a competency examination and an interview.

1. Open **HR settings** and go down to **Assessment templates**.
2. Under **Position categories**, rename a category, pick its **Default template**, or choose **Add a category**.
3. Under **Templates**, change a criterion's name, **What it rates**, **Kind** or **Weight (%)**. Use the arrows to reorder, and the bin to remove one. Choose **Add a criterion** or **Add a template** for more.
4. Pick how a template's ratings are combined under **Combine the assessors' ratings**: **Average of the assessors** (the default), **Average without the highest and lowest**, or **Board consensus** (see [Choose how the assessors' ratings are combined](#choose-how-the-assessors-ratings-are-combined)).
5. Check the total under the criteria: it must say *Total 100%*. It turns red when the weights do not add up to 100.
6. Under **Printed comparative list**, type the **Title**, the **Signature caption**, the **Name on the signature line** (or leave it blank to sign by hand) and the **Title under the line**, for example *HRMPSB Secretariat*. **Caption over the assessors' lines** (*Rated by*) and **Assessor's title under their line** (*Member, HRMPSB*) are for the selection board's signature lines.
7. Under **Selection board**, tick **Assessors see the combined result (total and rank) after submitting their own rating** if your school wants that. It is off by default: only HR sees the result.
8. Choose **Save assessment templates**.

ATLAS refuses a template whose weights do not total exactly 100%, and says *the weights total 101%. They must total exactly 100%.* Each criterion is scored from 0 to 100, so a weighted total (score × weight ÷ 100, added up) can never be more than 100. Weights can have two decimals (33.33 + 33.33 + 33.34). Changing a template gives it a new version; an opening already rated keeps the version it was rated with. A template or category that an opening uses cannot be removed: tick **Retired** on the template, or rename the category. Each change is in the Settings center's **Change history**.

![HR settings → Assessment templates](hr-assessment-templates.jpg)

### Choose how the assessors' ratings are combined

Each template says how the ratings of an opening's selection board become one result, criterion by criterion:

| Rule | What it does |
|---|---|
| Average of the assessors (the default) | Each criterion's score is the average of the assessors who have submitted. |
| Average without the highest and lowest | Each criterion's highest and lowest scores are left out and the rest averaged. Only with 3 or more scores; with 1 or 2 it is the plain average. |
| Board consensus | The secretariat (HR) enters one agreed score per criterion after the board meets; that is the result. The assessors' own scores stay for reference. |

1. Open **HR settings → Assessment templates**.
2. On the template, pick the rule under **Combine the assessors' ratings**.
3. Choose **Save assessment templates**.

Changing the rule gives the template a new version; an opening already rated keeps its own. Drafts never count; only submitted ratings do.

### Add a Performance rating criterion

For promotions, a criterion can be the applicant's performance rating. People has no performance evaluation (IPCR) yet, so each assessor types the score with its **Source** (for example *IPCR Jan–Jun 2026: Very Satisfactory*), or picks the adjectival rating from the template's list, which turns it into a score.

1. In the template, choose **Add a criterion** (or use one you have) and set its **Kind** to **Performance rating**. A template can have one.
2. Under **Adjectival ratings, out of 100**, check what each rating counts for. The values ATLAS starts with (Outstanding 100, Very Satisfactory 90, Satisfactory 75, Unsatisfactory 50, Poor 25) are *Example values*: replace them with your merit selection plan's.
3. Under **External applicants**, pick **Not applicable: its weight is spread over the other criteria** (the default) or **A typed score, e.g. a rating from a previous employer**.
4. Choose **Save assessment templates**.

With the default, an external applicant's rating shows the criterion as *Not applicable*, and the other criteria share its weight in proportion: with Performance rating 20%, Exam 40% and Interview 40%, an external applicant's total is (Exam × 40 + Interview × 40) ÷ 80.

### Name the selection board of an opening

The assessors are the selection board members who rate the applicants. Any active staff account can be one (a teacher, a dean, a staff member), not only HR. Naming them is what lets them see that opening's applicants, applications and documents, and nothing else.

1. Open the opening and go down to **Selection board**.
2. Choose **Name the assessors** (or **Change**).
3. Under **Add an assessor**, type a name, position or office and choose the person.
4. Type a title if you like, for example *Chairperson*. It prints under their signature line.
5. Choose **Save the board**.

Each new assessor is told on the bell and finds the opening under **Ratings to do**. Someone who applied for the opening cannot be on its board. An assessor who has submitted a rating stays on the board, because their ratings are part of the result; one who has not can be taken off (their drafts go). Every change is in the audit log.

![Name the selection board](hr-name-board.jpg)

### Rate an applicant

When an opening has a selection board, its assessors rate (see [the assessor's guide](assessor)); you follow their progress and, with **Board consensus**, enter the agreed scores. Without a board, HR rates, as the HR office.

1. Open the opening. The **Rating** column shows each applicant's combined total and rank, or *Not rated*, and *Rated by 2 of 3* while the board is rating.
2. On the applicant's row, choose **Rate** (or **Rating** to see one).
3. Read **Each assessor's rating**: every assessor's scores per criterion and the combined result. Drafts are not shown and not counted.
4. To rate for the HR office, type a **Score** from 0 to 100 for each criterion you have rated, and a **Comment** if you like. Leave a criterion blank until it has been rated, for example an interview still to come. On an opening with a board this is optional, and counts as one more assessor's rating.
5. Choose **Save rating**.

ATLAS keeps who rated and when; the HR office's rating is one shared rating, so another HR person's replaces it, with the earlier totals listed under the scores. Every rating goes to the audit log (*Rated a job application*, *Changed the rating of a job application*, *Saved a draft rating of a job application*), with each score before and after. The applicant never sees scores, comments or the rank. You cannot rate your own application. The first rating at an opening (even a draft) fixes its template: the opening keeps a copy of that version, so later changes to the template never change its ratings, and its position category and template can no longer be changed. When the last assessor submits, you are told on the bell.

![Rate an applicant: a score per criterion, the weighted total and the rank](hr-rate-applicant.jpg)

### Enter the board consensus

For a template that combines by **Board consensus**, the result is the score the board agrees per criterion, entered by the secretariat.

1. Open the opening and choose **Rating** on the applicant's row.
2. Under **Board consensus**, the boxes start with the average of the assessors who submitted. Type the agreed **Score** for each criterion.
3. Choose **Save the consensus**.

Until then the applicant is not on the comparative list (it says how many wait for the consensus). You can enter it before every assessor has rated, and change it later; the earlier totals are kept, and each change is in the audit log.

### Review an applicant's documents

Each opening has a checklist of documents (see [Post a job opening](#post-a-job-opening)). Applicants upload against each item, and you check them.

1. Open the opening. The **Rating** column shows *Complete* or *Missing 2 required* for each application, with how many files are waiting for you (*1 to check*). Choose **Incomplete documents** above the list to see only those.
2. Choose the badge on the applicant's row. **Documents of** the applicant opens.
3. Choose a file to download it and read it.
4. Choose **Accept**, or **Needs a new file…**, type a note to the applicant (what is wrong and what to upload instead) and choose **Ask and tell the applicant**.

The applicant is told on the bell, from *HR office*, and sees the status and your note beside the document. A new file they upload makes the item *Uploaded* again, for you to check, and you are told on the bell. **Clear the review** undoes your mark. Until the opening closes the applicant can replace or remove their files; after it closes they can only upload a file you asked for. Every review and every download is in the audit log.

![HR asks for a new file](hr-needs-new-file.jpg)

### Set the applicant upload policy and the default checklists

1. Open **HR settings** and go down to **Applicant documents**.
2. Under **Upload policy**, tick the **File types allowed** (PDF, Word, Excel and pictures by default), the **Largest file (MB)** (2 by default, 1 to 10) and the **Most files per application** (15 by default).
3. Change the **Help on compressing and splitting files** if you like: applicants see it beside the upload buttons and with a file that is too large.
4. Under **Default checklists**, edit each position category's list of documents. The built-in lists are examples.
5. Choose **Save applicant documents**.

A new opening copies its category's list; an existing opening keeps its own. ATLAS works out what each file is from the file itself, never its name, and always refuses programs, scripts, web pages, Office files with macros and password-protected files.

![HR settings → Applicant documents](hr-applicant-docs-settings.jpg)

### See and print the comparative list

1. Open the opening and choose **Comparative list**.
2. Read the rated applicants, ranked by their combined result (equal totals share a rank), with a column per criterion, how many assessors rated each, and the stage. *n/a* marks a performance rating that does not apply to an external applicant.
3. Read **Each assessor's total** under it (HR only; it is not printed).
4. Choose **Export CSV** for a spreadsheet (with each assessor's total), or **Print**.

The printout is A4 landscape, with the school's name and seal, the title from **Assessment templates**, the opening, the template, how ratings are combined and *As of* today's date. The headings repeat on every page. At the end there is a signature line for each assessor on the selection board, with their title, and one for the HRMPSB secretariat. Withdrawn applications are left out; applications not rated yet, or waiting for the board consensus, are counted above the list.

### Open and print an applicant profile

1. Choose an applicant's name on an opening, or **Profile** in **Recruitment → Applicant accounts**, or a name in **Pool of applicants**.
2. Read the **Applicant profile**: name and contact, what they gave at sign-up, the account's dates, the pool, and every application at your campuses with its opening, stage, outcome, documents and history of notes.
3. To include them, tick **Include internal remarks** (HR's remarks and the reasons for moving back) and **Include ratings** (the scores, total and rank).
4. Choose **Print**.

The profile prints on A4 with the school header, *Applicant profile*, the date printed and who printed it. For an employee who applied, it shows their position, salary grade, appointment and campus from the employee record, with a link to the record if you may see it. People who ticked *Keep me in the school's pool of applicants* enter the pool when an application of theirs ends **Not selected** or **Not qualified**. They stay 12 months unless the school changed it.

1. Open **Recruitment → Pool of applicants**.
2. Type a name, an email or an opening in **Search**, or pick a **Position category**, an **Education (at least)**, a **Preferred campus**, a **Best rating** range or the dates they **Entered the pool**.
3. Choose **Filter**.

1. In **Pool of applicants**, choose **Invite to apply** on their row.
2. Pick the **Opening**: an open opening at your campuses (an internal-only one only for employees).
3. Type a **Note** if you like.
4. Choose **Send the invitation**.

### Extend or remove an applicant account

People outside the school apply with a temporary **applicant account** (see [the applicant's guide](applicant)). It closes a set number of days after sign-up (365 unless changed under **HR settings → Job applicants**); the applicant is told on the bell before.

1. Open **Recruitment → Applicant accounts**. Each account shows what the applicant gave at sign-up, when they signed up, until when it is open (or when a closed one will be removed), and how many applications it has.
2. To give someone more time, choose **Extend**, type the **Days to add** and choose **Extend**. The days are added to the closing date (or to today, if it has passed), a closed account opens again, and the applicant is told.
3. To remove an account now (for example when the person asks), choose **Remove** and confirm.

Removing an account, by hand or by the daily removal after it closed, takes away its sign-in and the personal details in its applications. Each application stays in the opening's list as *Removed applicant*, without a name, so the counts stay true; its rating keeps its scores (without comments) so the comparative list stays true, and the person leaves the pool. Extending and removing are in the audit log.

An applicant in the pool of applicants keeps their account open until the end of their time in the pool, and is told so on the bell. When that time ends, they leave the pool and the account closes and is removed as usual.

## Questions

**Why is someone counted absent when they were at work?**
A scheduled work day with no attendance record counts as an absence. Add the record in **Attendance (DTR)** before payroll is run, or ask the payroll officer to recompute a draft run afterwards.

**Can I approve my own leave?**
No. Another HR manager or an administrator must approve it.

**A sick leave cannot be approved. Why?**
If the leave type needs a medical certificate for that many days (Sick Leave: more than 3 work days), it must be attached first. The employee attaches it under **Leave & requests → Attach certificate** while the request is pending.

**Does a clock-in away from campus stop the employee?**
No. Locations are recorded, never used to block. Treat a flag as a reason to check: laptops on Wi-Fi can be off by a few hundred metres, and a location can be faked. The payroll officer and the employee do not see locations.

**What does FLEX mean on a record?**
Flexible time: clock-in and clock-out times do not affect pay, so there is no late or undertime. Absences and approved overtime still count.

**What happens to leave already in a finalized payroll?**
Payslips of finalized runs never change. If you record or remove leave on a day that is already paid, ATLAS tells you, and the correction has to be made in a later payroll.

**Where do the default leave types come from?**
Common Philippine practice: Service Incentive Leave of at least 5 days (Labor Code), maternity (RA 11210), paternity (RA 8187) and solo parent leave (RA 11861). Adjust them to school policy under **Leave types**.

**Why is tardiness taken from vacation leave?**
Under government payroll, tardiness and undertime are charged to vacation leave credits first (CSC Omnibus Rules on Leave) when the payroll run is finalized; what the credits cannot cover is deducted from pay. Payroll setup can change this to *Deducted from pay*.

**Does a plantilla item make an employee permanent?**
No. The item and the status of appointment are separate: linking an item never changes the appointment, and ATLAS never works out permanence from the item. A temporary or casual appointee can hold an item; the appointment on their record says what it is.

**Where do item numbers come from?**
From DBM. ATLAS records them exactly as given and never makes one up, and each number can be in the register once. ATLAS says *Item number … is already in the register* when it is typed a second time.

**Why can I not link a job order to an item?**
Job orders and contracts of service are not on the plantilla. Someone who holds an item cannot be changed to a job order either: unlink them first.

**Why is the authorized salary different from the actual one?**
The authorized annual salary is the item's: its salary grade at step 1, × 12. The actual annual salary is the incumbent's: their own salary grade and step, from their record, × 12.

**What does "To review" mean on an item?**
The item was recorded by ATLAS from an item number typed on an employee record before the register, or more than one record names it. Check it against DBM's list and mark it as reviewed (see [Review items recorded from employee records](#review-items-recorded-from-employee-records)).

**Who can use Plantilla?**
HR managers and administrators keep it (the *Plantilla* permission). Anyone who can view employee records, such as the payroll officer, can read it, export it and print the PSIPOP. An administrator can give the permission to another account type under **Roles & permissions**.

**Who sees an opening?**
An opening is shown only while its status is **Open** and until its closing date. **Open to all**: people outside the school on the ATLAS home page, and every employee in People. **Internal only**: employees only; applicants never see it, even by its address.

**How is an internal application different?**
An employee applies with their own account from **Me → Job openings**: there is no second account. The application is marked **Internal** and linked to their employee record, so you see their current position, salary grade and campus. Employee accounts never close like applicant accounts.

**Why does an opening count a "Removed applicant"?**
An applicant account is removed some days after it closes (**HR settings → Job applicants**), or when you remove it. An applicant you marked **Selected** is never removed automatically, because their details may become part of a personnel record; remove the account yourself when it is no longer needed. The application stays, without the person's details, so the number of applications stays true.

**What does the applicant see of an application?**
The stages (done, current and to come, with their dates), what happens at the current stage (the description you set in **Recruitment stages**), the notes you wrote to them in time order, and the outcome once decided. Every note comes from *HR office*. They never see internal remarks, the reasons for moving back, or who in HR moved it.

**Are the default stages the school's merit selection plan?**
They follow the usual public-sector merit selection flow: application received, initial screening against the qualification standards, assessment (examination or interview), deliberation by the selection board, and the appointing authority's final decision. Check them against your school's own merit selection plan, and rename, add or retire stages under **HR settings → Recruitment stages** to match it.

**What happened to Received, Under review and Shortlisted?**
Applications from before the recruitment stages were moved to them: *Received* to the first stage, *Under review* to the second (*Initial screening*), *Shortlisted* to *Assessment (examination or interview)*, and *Selected*, *Not selected* and *Withdrawn* to the same outcomes. Their history, with the dates, the names and the notes, came with them.

**Can the applicant see their scores?**
No. Applicants see the stages, your notes and the outcome, never scores, comments, totals or ranks. Some merit selection processes publish the results of the comparative assessment to the applicants; if your school's does, give them the results the way your merit selection plan says.

**Where do the default assessment templates come from?**
They are examples, with plausible criteria and weights, so you can try ATLAS. Replace them under **HR settings → Assessment templates** with the criteria and weights of your school's merit selection plan, for faculty and for non-teaching positions.

**ATLAS says "the weights total 101%. They must total exactly 100%."** The weights of a template must add up to exactly 100. Change one of them so the total under the criteria says *Total 100%*, then save again.

**Why can't I change the template of this opening?**
An application to it has been rated. The opening keeps the template version it was rated with, so every applicant to it is rated the same way.

**Can several board members rate the same applicant?**
Not yet: one rating per application, by one person. Record the board's agreed scores.

**Who is in the pool of applicants?**
Only people who ticked *Keep me in the school's pool of applicants* themselves, at sign-up or under **My applications**, and whose application then ended **Not selected** or **Not qualified** (or whom you added by hand, with a reason). It is never ticked for them. They stay the months set under **HR settings → Job applicants** (12 unless changed), never longer than they agreed to, and can leave at any time.

**Who can use Recruitment?**
HR managers and administrators (the *Recruitment: job openings, applications and applicant accounts* permission). An administrator can give it to another account type under **Roles & permissions**.

**Can the assessors see each other's scores?**
Not before they submit their own. After submitting, they see only their own, unless **HR settings → Assessment templates → Assessors see the combined result** is ticked: then they also see the combined total and rank. Only HR ever sees each assessor's scores.

**What does "Average without the highest and lowest" do with two assessors?**
It is the plain average. The highest and lowest score of a criterion are left out only when 3 or more assessors scored it.

**Who can be an assessor?**
Any active staff account HR names on an opening's selection board: HR, teachers, deans, staff. Naming them is what gives them access, to that opening only (the *hris.assess* permission, which no account type has built in). An administrator can stop one account from rating anywhere by taking *hris.assess* away from it under **Users & Access → Access**.

**What happened to ratings made before selection boards?**
Each became the HR office's rating, by the person who rated, submitted when it was made. Totals and ranks are unchanged.

**What happens to applicants' documents later?**
They go with the applicant's account: when the account is removed after it closes, or when you remove it, its files are deleted. A **Selected** applicant's account, and so their files, is never removed automatically. When someone leaves the pool of applicants, the files of their applications that ended (not selected, not qualified, withdrawn) are deleted. Files uploaded for an opening someone never applied to are deleted once it stops taking applications.

**Where are applicants' documents kept?**
Encrypted, beside ATLAS's data (*applicant-files*), with a key derived from the server key, as ATLAS Documents keeps its files. They open only for the applicant, HR at the opening's campus and its assessors, through ATLAS; there is no public link.
