## Before you start

This guide is for parents and guardians. ATLAS Learn shows you the students the school linked to your account, and for each one only what the registrar's office chose to share with you. You can look; you cannot change anything.

What you need:

- Your **username and password** from the registrar's office. If the password was set by someone else, ATLAS asks you to choose a new one the first time.
- A link to your student. If you sign in and see *No students linked to your account yet*, contact the registrar's office.

To sign in:

1. Open the ATLAS home page and sign in.
2. Under **Your systems**, choose **Learn**.

Your name at the top right opens a menu with **Change password** and **Sign out**. The **?** button opens these guides.

## How to…

### Check how your student is doing this term

1. In the sidebar, choose **Your students**.
2. Read the student's card: running grade, attendance rate, enrolled units and activities due.
3. Look at the lines under the figures. ATLAS flags a pending enrollment request, absences, a class below the passing grade, and work not handed in after the due date.
4. Choose **Open** for the details.

### See grades

1. Open the student and choose **Grades**.
2. For each class: the **Running grade** (in red when it is below the passing grade), **Work done** (for example *3 of 4*), and the **Final grade** with *Passed* or *Failed*.

The running grade counts the work scored so far, weighted as the school sets. Teachers give the final grade.

### See attendance

1. Open the student and choose **Attendance**.
2. Each class shows its attendance rate and how many days your student was present, late, absent or excused, with the latest days marked.

Late counts as attended; excused days are left out of the rate.

### See what is due and what is missing

1. Open the student and choose **Activities & quizzes**.
2. Read the label on the right of each activity (see below).

| Label | What it means |
|---|---|
| *Due …* | Open now, not handed in yet |
| *Handed in*, *Handed in late*, *Handed in · being checked* | Waiting for the teacher's score |
| *Scored 18/20* | The teacher's score |
| *Past due · late work accepted* | The due date has passed, but your student can still hand it in |
| *Missing* | The due date has passed and nothing was handed in |
| *Opens …* | Not open yet |

You see the titles, dates and scores, never the questions or answers.

### See enrollment and the class schedule

1. Open the student and choose **Enrollment**. It lists this term's requests and classes with their status (*Pending approval*, *Enrolled*, *Waitlisted* with the place in line…), the units, and the latest update. The total of enrolled units is at the bottom.
2. Choose **Class schedule** for the week's timetable.

### Look at an earlier term

1. Open the student.
2. Under **Term**, pick the term. The tabs then show that term.

The **Grades** tab always lists every term, so the term picker is not shown there.

### See the school calendar

**School calendar** shows, for each of your students, their class meetings and what is due (as far as each link shares **Class schedule** and **Activities & quizzes**), with examinations, holidays, days without classes, school events (including meetings for parents) and the term's dates.

1. Open **School calendar**. With more than one student, pick one, or **All my students** (each entry then starts with the student's name, e.g. *Carlo: Quiz 1*).
2. Choose **Month**, **Week** or **List**, and move with **‹**, **›** and **Today**.
3. Choose an entry for its details. A due date says whether the work is **Handed in**, **To do**, **Overdue** or **Missing**; **Open the activity** or **Open the quiz** (for a class meeting, **Open the class**) goes to the student's page.
4. The coloured tick boxes above the calendar show or hide each kind of entry.

To follow it from your phone, choose **Subscribe → Make my calendar address** and add the address to your calendar app, which then reads the calendar's own address, which keeps it up to date every few hours. Anyone with the address sees your calendar: if you shared it by mistake, choose **Get a new address** (the old one stops working) or **Stop sharing**.

![A parent's school calendar for two students](parent-calendar.jpg)

### Read school news and events

Open **News & events** for the school's announcements and upcoming events. You can react to a post with **Like**, **Love** or **Celebrate**, and write a comment and choose **Post**.

## Questions

**Why can't I see my student's grades (or another tab)?**
The registrar's office chooses, for each link between you and a student, what you can see: enrollment, class schedule, grades, attendance, and activities and quizzes, one by one. The school can also switch a view off for all parents. **What you can see** on the student's **Overview** lists what is shared with you. Ask the registrar's office to change it.

**Why can't I see my other child?**
Each student is linked to your account separately. Ask the registrar's office to link the other student.

**Can I change anything, or message the teacher here?**
No. Learn is read-only for parents and guardians. The class page and its lessons, resources and announcements are for students and teachers. Contact the teacher or the school directly.

**Does my student know I can see their records?**
Yes. Students have a **Parents & guardians** page that lists everyone linked to them, what each can see, and when each last looked.

**What is the running grade?**
It is the grade so far, from the work the teacher has scored. Each kind of activity (assignments, quizzes, exams) counts by the weight the school sets, and kinds with nothing scored yet are left out. The final grade is given by the teacher at the end of the term; 75 or higher passes unless your school set a different passing grade.
