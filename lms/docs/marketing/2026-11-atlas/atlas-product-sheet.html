Technical product sheetVersion 0.8.0-alpha · November 2026

One platform for the classroom, the registrar, HR and government accounting.

Lancaster ATLAS is a school platform for Philippine state universities and colleges. Students, parents, faculty and staff sign in once and open the systems their account may use: Learn, Academic, People, Finance and Documents. Payroll and accounting follow the rules a national government agency works under. The whole platform runs on a server the school controls, either its own or one on AWS.

01The product family One row per product: who uses it and what it does

ATLAS Learn LMS Live

Used by Teachers, students, parents and guardians; the registrar through its admin panel

  • Lessons, materials, assignments, and timed quizzes and exams that the server closes on time
  • Enrollment that checks prerequisites, the unit limit and schedule conflicts, with waitlists
  • Attendance, a weighted gradebook and final grades, with CSV export
  • Read-only parent accounts: the registrar chooses what each link shares

ATLAS Academic UIS Live · release 1

Used by The registrar's office, deans, teachers (grade sheets), students (own record)

  • One student record: evaluation against the curriculum, back subjects, units earned and GWA
  • Grade sheets go teacher, dean, registrar; a grade change needs a second person's approval
  • TOR, COR and certificates printed with a QR code that anyone can check
  • CHED enrollment list, FHE billing details, dean's list and candidates for graduation

ATLAS People HRIS Live

Used by The HR office, the payroll office, and every employee (self-service)

  • Employee records, a time clock and the daily time record (DTR), with late, undertime and overtime
  • Leave balances, requests and approvals; HR cannot approve their own leave
  • Government payroll, with the General Payroll (COA GAM Appendix 33) and a remittance summary per agency
  • Self-service for every employee: clock in, file leave, open payslips

ATLAS Finance FMIS Live · release 1

Used by The accounting, budget, cashier's and supply offices, and the approving officer

  • Revised Chart of Accounts, journal entry vouchers, ledgers, trial balance and statements per fund cluster
  • Appropriations, allotments and NCAs; ORS/BURS with Box B checking the allotment
  • Disbursement vouchers through Boxes A to E, checks and LDDAP-ADA, cash advances, BIR Form 2307
  • Official receipts, the RCD and deposits; purchase orders, stock cards, PAR/ICS and depreciation

ATLAS Documents Live

Used by Every employee and every student

  • Your own files, office and college folders, and what others share with you
  • Every version kept, with check-out while a file is being edited
  • Sharing at view, edit or manage; outside links with a password, an end date and a download limit
  • Every file encrypted on the server; programs, scripts and Office files with macros are refused

PDF Studio Tools Live

Used by The school's employees; an administrator chooses which account types

  • Turns Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OpenDocument files and pictures into PDF; files up to 100 MB
  • Puts PDFs together; reorders, turns, removes and numbers pages
  • Changes a line of text, adds text, and signs
  • A signature record page with a code; a check says whether a signed PDF is unchanged

ATLAS Facilities Coming soon

  • Rooms and venues, bookings, maintenance and property. Not yet available; its tile shows greyed out on the home page.

02One front door

The home page

A card for each system the account may use, with figures from that person's own work, then the school's announcements and upcoming events.

One sign-in

Sign in once and open each system without signing in again. Sign out anywhere signs out of everything. The school's Keycloak account can be used instead of a password.

Campuses

Every campus has the same features, catalog and grading, and its own students, teachers, sections, news and events. Staff at more than one campus switch from their name.

Notifications

A bell in every system: an enrollment decided, a grade sheet to approve, a leave request to decide, a payslip ready, a voucher waiting for your step.

03Built for Philippine government rules

COA · DBM · BIR

Finance

  • COA Government Accounting Manual and Revised Chart of Accounts; one set of books per fund cluster
  • DBM budget execution: ORS for fund 01 and BURS for the others; SAAODB (FAR 1), RAOD and aging of obligations (FAR 3)
  • The server enforces separation of duties: Box D is never signed by the preparer or the certifier
  • BIR withholding on government payments, remittances by TRA, the quarterly alphalist and Form 2307
CSC · GSIS · PHILHEALTH · PAG-IBIG · BIR

Payroll, in People

  • Salary grade and step under EO 64, s. 2024; the 2026 and 2027 schedules are built in
  • PERA and RATA; GSIS at 9% personal and 12% government share; PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG and BIR withholding tax
  • Tardiness and undertime charged to vacation leave credits first (CSC), the rest from pay
  • Loans held back when net pay would fall below ₱5,000 a month; mid-year and year-end bonus, cash gift, PEI and clothing allowance runs
  • A finalized run goes to Finance: its ORS/BURS, the payroll voucher, then the remittance vouchers
CHED

Academic

  • CHED enrollment list
  • FHE billing details (UniFAST Form 2), with lecture and laboratory units
  • Enrollment summary by program, year and sex
  • Reports print on A4 and export to CSV

Every Finance form prints on A4 after its GAM appendix. Tax rates, contribution tables and the chart of accounts can be edited by the school, and are checked against the documents its accountant and COA auditor use before going live.

04Security and privacy controls

  • Passwords are stored only as hashes, and each hash is encrypted under a key kept outside the data.
  • 2-step verification with an authenticator app and recovery codes. Administrators choose who must use it.
  • Access by account type. The server refuses what a role may not do; it is not only hidden. One person can be given, or lose, a single permission.
  • Tamper-evident audit log. Sign-ins, failed sign-ins and staff changes are numbered and chained by SHA-256, and the page re-checks the chain.
  • Security monitor for administrators: refused requests by kind, failed sign-ins, traffic and who is signed in.
  • Rate limits per person and per network address. Five wrong passwords lock a username for five minutes.
  • Sessions. People are signed out after 8 idle hours, and can see and sign out their other devices.
  • Privacy by role. Parents see only what each link shares, and the student sees who is linked. A leave's reason and medical certificate are shown to HR only.

05Architecture and deployment

Software
One Node.js program with no third-party dependencies: a small attack surface, and code that is easy to audit.
Hosting
The school's own server, or AWS. One CloudFormation template builds the EC2 server with HTTPS and a fixed IP.
Clients
Any modern browser, on phones too. No app to install.
Sign-in
Single sign-on with Keycloak or another standard OpenID Connect provider.
Updates
On the AWS setup, updates install by themselves, and only if the tests pass on the server.
Backups
On the AWS setup, a copy of the data every night and a snapshot of the disk every day.
Network
Can sit behind Cloudflare at a name on the school's own domain.
Data
Records are kept in one data file on the server, with uploaded files beside it. Sized with you for your enrollment during onboarding.

06What's included

  • Built-in guides. A Help button in every system opens the guide for that person's job, with screenshots and how-to steps. Guides are kept current with every release, and the school can edit them.
  • Onboarding. First-run setup names the school and its campus and creates the administrator. The school sets its own colour and logo. The server is sized with you for your enrollment.
  • Demo school data. A demo school with several campuses, payroll runs and this fiscal year's books. It is added next to your own data and removed again without changing your records.
